Volunteer Training with SVPRO

UBC Okanagan Events

Join the Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office (SVPRO) team as a Consent Ambassador Participate in meaningful events and campaigns that foster consent cultures on campus. As a volunteer, you will receive an afternoon of training on consent and how to respond to disclosures. We provide coffee, tea and a full lunch! Volunteering is open to all UBCO students. There is NO minimum time commitment, so you get to decide what works best for you! We offer three volunteer training days throughout the school year.
