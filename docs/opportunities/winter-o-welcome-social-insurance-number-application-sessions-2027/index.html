Winter O Welcome: Social Insurance Number Application Sessions

UBC Okanagan Events

A Social Insurance Number (SIN) is a nine-digit number issued by Service Canada, a Canadian government department. You need a SIN before starting work in Canada to file taxes, as well as to access government programs and benefits, if eligible. You will also need to provide your SIN to UBC for tax purposes. The Global Engagement Office team will be hosting representatives from Service Canada on Thursday, January 7, to help you apply for your social insurance number on-site. First come, first served. Note: You must bring physical copies of your passport and study permit to the session.
