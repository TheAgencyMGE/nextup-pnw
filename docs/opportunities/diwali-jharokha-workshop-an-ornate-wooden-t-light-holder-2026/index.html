Diwali Jharokha Workshop: An Ornate Wooden T-light Holder

King County Library System

Create and decorate a beautiful wooden Jharokha-style T-light candle holder inspired by the ornate windows of traditional Indian palaces and havelis. In Indian culture, the jharokha symbolizes beauty, heritage, and connection, making it a timeless architectural element. All ages are welcome. Children must be accompanied by an adult. Registration is encouraged. Location: Crossroads Community Center, 16000 NE 10th St, Bellevue, WA 98008Hosted by Deepti Designs Inc.
