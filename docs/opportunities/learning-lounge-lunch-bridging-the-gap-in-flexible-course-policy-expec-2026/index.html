Learning Lounge @ Lunch: Bridging the Gap in Flexible Course Policy Expectations (HYBRID)

Boise State University Events

Join us for lunch and a conversation with Drs. David Hunt and Anne Hamby (Department of Marketing) about student and instructor perspectives on flexible grading policies. Drawing on college-wide survey findings, they will highlight places where student expectations and instructor perspectives aligned, and places where they diverged substantially. Together, participants will explore why these gaps emerge, what instructors have done to better communicate and design course policies, and how flexible policies can support student learning while remaining manageable and equitable.
