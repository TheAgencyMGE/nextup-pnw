Great Group Work: Setting Students Up for Success

Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology

Group assignments can be powerful tools for collaborative learning—but they often come with challenges, from uneven participation to unclear expectations. This interactive, one-hour workshop is designed to help instructors explore how to design, manage, and assess group assignments more effectively. By the end of this session, you will be able to: Reflect on current research findings about why students may resist group work Consider how communication, transparency, clear expectations, and value can encourage student experience in group work Use practical strategies, real-world examples, and guided discussion so instructors can (or not!) use group assignments to fulfil their teaching goals Wh
