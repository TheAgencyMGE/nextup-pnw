Ignite Children's Environmental Health Research Seminar: Keep Kids Safe from Lead

Oregon State University Events

October is Children’s Environmental Health Month! To honor Children’s Environmental Health Month and National Lead Poisoning Prevention Week (October 25-31, 2026) the Oregon State University ASPIRE Children's Environmental Health Center is hosting an Ignite research seminar highlighing the ongoing need to keep kids safe from lead!
