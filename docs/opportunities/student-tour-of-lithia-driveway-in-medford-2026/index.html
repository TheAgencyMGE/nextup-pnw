Student Tour of Lithia & Driveway in Medford

Southern Oregon University Events

This is a great opportunity to tour the headquarters in Medford for Lithia & Driveway (LAD). You will have the opportunity to see what it is like working for LAD and the types of jobs that they offer. You can also find out about applying for summer internships at LAD. You must sign up ahead of time for this tour. Research the company before the tour… Career Connections will contact you a few days prior to this tour to confirm the details. Why go on a tour?
