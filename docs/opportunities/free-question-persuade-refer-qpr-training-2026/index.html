FREE Question, Persuade, Refer (QPR) Training

Oregon State University Events

Develop the skills to give emergency response to someone in crisis. This training will help you: Recognize the warning signs and risk factors for suicide. Intervene and ask questions to assess the situation. Refer someone at risk of suicide to an appropriate resource. Question, Persuade, Refer (QPR) is an innovative and proven suicide prevention program. This training empowers each of us to make a positive difference in the lives of others.
