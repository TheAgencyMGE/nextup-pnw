Kelownafornia: Moviescapes of the BC Interior

UBC Okanagan Events

For over a century, filmmakers have looked at the Okanagan and seen somewhere else. This fall, we bring home nine movies—indie, Canadian and big-budget Hollywood—to celebrate the landscape hiding in plain sight behind every place it’s ever played. Come see the Okanagan Valley through the films that turned it into somewhere else. This festival is presented as part of The Kelownafornia Project, centred around researching how people in the Okanagan perceive their natural environment through various themes and aspects.
