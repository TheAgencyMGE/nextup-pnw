Information Session: M.A. Policy Studies (online)

UW Bothell Events

Interested in using policy to address complex social issues and create meaningful change? Join us to learn more about the Master of Arts in Policy Studies (MAPS) at the University of Washington Bothell. MAPS offers an interdisciplinary, evidence-based approach to policy, preparing students to apply policy analysis, data, evaluation, and social theory to real-world challenges. Designed with working professionals in mind, the program follows a cohort model with evening classes and opportunities to work on community-based projects.
