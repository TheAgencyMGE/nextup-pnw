UBC CPD: 24th Annual St. Paul’s Emergency Medicine Update

Continuing Professional Development (UBC CPD)

Join us this September for the 24th St. Paul’s Emergency Medicine Update! The Whistler Conference Centre will once again host a weekend of engaging emergency medicine education and the opportunity to connect with colleagues in one of BC’s most beautiful settings. Set in the mountains of Whistler, this is emergency medicine education at its best. The program features hands-on workshops, case-based seminars, and high-yield talks that focus on the clinical challenges faced in the ED. Alongside new topics, we’re also bringing back some favourite sessions from past years. We hope you’ll join us in Whistler for a memorable and practical learning experience.
