2026 Vancouver Law Fair

UBC Peter A. Allard School of Law

Visit the Vancouver Law School Fair at Jack Poole Hall, in the Robert H. Lee Alumni Centre at the University of British Columbia on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 between 2:00pm – 6:30pm to learn about your law school options and meet Allard Law admissions advisors. This is a free event for those interested in learning more about law school. Chat one-on-one with representatives from 20+ law schools from across North America, LSAT prep organizations, current and aspiring law students on topics such as: Law school admission requirements. Scholarships, awards and financing your legal education. The student experience at law school. Career opportunities Undergraduate support The process of applying
