Beav-Eats: BNC Weekly Food and Cooking Workshop

Oregon State University Events

Join the Basic Needs Center for Beav-Eats , a weekly drop-in workshop series focused on building food skills, confidence and community. Each week explores a different topic related to cooking, meal planning, prepping, grocery shopping, food budgeting and more. Whether you're new to cooking or looking to expand your skills, Beav-Eats offers a welcoming space to learn practical strategies, try new things and connect with fellow students. No experience required. Weekly Topics Schedule: Week 3 - Packing Lunch Boxes Week 4 - Ways to Cook Eggs Week 5 - Easy and Simple Desserts Week 6 - Ways to Cook Potatoes Week 7 - One Pan Meals Week 8 - Meal Prep Week 9 - To be announced Week 10 - To be announce
