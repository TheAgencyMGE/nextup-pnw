WA State Legislative Internship Winter 2027 Info Session

UW Bothell Events

Come learn about the Washington State Legislative Internship Program , a hands-on opportunity to spend a legislative session working alongside state lawmakers and gaining real-world experience in government, research, communication, and the legislative process. Students from all majors are encouraged to explore this opportunity. Whether you’re interested in public service, law, communications, business, education—or just want an unforgettable professional experience—come to our info session to learn what the internship is like, who’s eligible, and how to apply. Last year, 6 UW Bothell interns were accepted (out of 70 interns across the state!). Deadline to apply for the internship is Oct. 28
