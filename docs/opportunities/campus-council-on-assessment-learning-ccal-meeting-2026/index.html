Campus Council on Assessment & Learning (CCAL) Meeting

UW Bothell Events

If you would like to attend, please email Dawn Moncalieri, GFO Program Coordinator, at dawn3@uw.edu to request the zoom link. *** An agenda will be added here one week prior to the meeting. *** The Campus Council on Assessment and Learning (CCAL) serves as an advisory board to the GFO Executive Council regarding implementation, revision, and assessment of campus-level student learning goals and proficiencies. This council meets monthly and consists of a faculty representative from each of the 5 schools and FYPP as well as ex-officio representatives from various UWB units.
