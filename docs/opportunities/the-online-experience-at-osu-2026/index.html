The Online Experience at OSU

Oregon State University Events

Are you curious about what it’s like to be an online student at Oregon State University? Join us for an engaging webinar where we’ll explore the tools, resources and support systems that make OSU’s online learning experience exceptional. Designed for prospective students, this session will provide valuable insights into how OSU ensures student success in a digital environment. In this interactive webinar, you’ll: 1) Learn about the key services available to online students, including academic support, career coaching, and engagement opportunities. 2) Hear firsthand experiences from current students about their journey in OSU’s online programs. 3) Discover best practices for staying connected
