Employer On-Campus: Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)

UBC Career Centre

AMD will be hosting an information session with UBC students, with a particular focus on 3rd-year students interested in the disciplines where we typically hire, including engineering, computer science, software development, hardware design, AI, and related technical fields. During the session, we’ll provide an overview of AMD, the innovative technologies we develop, the types of student opportunities and roles we offer, and the skills that help candidates succeed. Attendees will also have the opportunity to connect with AMD representatives from a variety of technical and professional backgrounds, learn about different career paths, and gain firsthand insights into working at AMD.
