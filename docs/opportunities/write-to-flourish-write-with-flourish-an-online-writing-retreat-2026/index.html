Write to Flourish, Write with Flourish: An Online Writing Retreat

Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication

This November, join the Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication for an online writing retreat as part of UBC’s Thrive campaign, and connect with others across UBC as you write. Whether you’re working on a dissertation, research article, grant proposal, course paper, or any other project, this is your time for dedicated writing. Our structured sessions, shared goal-setting, and peer support are designed to help you stay focused , build momentum , and leave feeling accomplished , from wherever you choose to write. This event is part of UBC Thrive. Explore Your Path to Mental Health. #UBCThrive Facilitator: Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication Location: Zoom (link sent one day
