Honors College Fall Undergraduate Research Showcase

Oregon State University Events

The Oregon State University Honors College invites you to attend the Fall Undergraduate Research Showcases. Separate showcases will be held over Zoom on Tuesday, October 27 and Tuesday, November 17 at 5 p.m. Pacific Time. At these online events, current honors students will present brief, accessible talks about their research, scholarship or creative work and answer questions. Each showcase will feature a different group of presenters. RSVP for one showcase or both.
