Preserving Learning in an Era of Automation

UW Tacoma Events

Join Marc Watkins, coauthor of the new Norton Guide to AI-Aware Teaching , to explore strategies for balancing analog learning and technologies like AI to protect students’ core skills development. The University is committed to providing access, equal opportunity, and reasonable accommodation in its services, programs, activities, education, and employment for individuals with disabilities. These events will include live-captioning services. If you need additional disability accommodations, please reach out to the UW Disability Services Office (DSO). When contacting DSO at dso@uw.edu , please share the event details listed in the event registration form.
