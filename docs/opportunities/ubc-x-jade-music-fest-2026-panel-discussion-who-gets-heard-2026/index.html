UBC x Jade Music Fest 2026 | Panel Discussion: Who Gets Heard?

UBC Hong Kong Studies Initiative

Join UBC Hong Kong Studies Initiative and Jade Music Fest for “Who Gets Heard?”, a panel discussion exploring language, accents, and meaningful representation for artists in Canada. Featuring Vancouver-based music artists Caroline Ding, Daniel Lew, and hip-hop artist Scope G alongside CreativeBC’s Joseph Bardsley, moderated by Dr. Helena Wu (UBC Department of Asian Studies). Date: Wed, 04 November 2026 Time: 12:00 – 14:15 Venue: Coach House, Green College, UBC The event will be conducted in English; free admission with registration: https://hksi.ubc.ca/events/event/jmf2026_panel/
