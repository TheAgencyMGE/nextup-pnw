UBC CPD: 20th Annual Pacific Psychopharmacology Conference

Continuing Professional Development (UBC CPD)

We’re pleased to announce this year’s 20th Annual Pacific Psychopharmacology Conference is returning to the Pinnacle Hotel Harbourfront in Vancouver, BC on Friday, Sept. 18, 2026 . We have an incredible group of international and local experts joining us to discuss topics such as: Treatment and Management of OCD and OCD-related Disorders Approaches to Managing Co-morbid Psychiatric and Cardiac Illnesses Management of Complex Opioid Withdrawal Syndrome Pharmacologic Management of Somatic Symptom and Related Disorders Treatment Guidelines for Mood & Anxiety Disorders in Perimenopause/Menopause and more! New for 2026 Virtual participants now have live access to all 6 concurrent breakout session
