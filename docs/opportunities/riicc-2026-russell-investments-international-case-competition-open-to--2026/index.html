RIICC 2026 | Russell Investments International Case Competition [Open to UW undergraduates]

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Russell Investments International Case Competition (RIICC) 2026 Join the Foster School of Business Global Business Center's annual Russell Investments International Case Competition (RIICC) on November 14, 2026! RIICC brings together UW students to tackle a global business challenge and present solutions to Seattle business professionals. Not competing? UW undergraduates can also volunteer to help with event operations and support teams throughout the day. Interested in competing? Team Registration Deadline: October 26, 2026 Interested in volunteering? Volunteer Sign-Up Deadline: November 2, 2026 Open to all UW undergraduate students on the UW Seattle campus.
