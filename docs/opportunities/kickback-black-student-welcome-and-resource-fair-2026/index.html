Kickback: Black student welcome and resource fair

UBC Okanagan Events

Join us for the fourth annual Kickback event in the Courtyard on Thursday, September 17, from 3 to 6 pm. Hosted by the Black Student Success Program , Kickback is our annual event welcoming folks to campus! Stop by and mingle with new and returning students, grab a bite to eat and enjoy music, explore resources at our resource fair (new for 2026!), play lawn games, and more. This event is open to all Black-identifying undergraduate and graduate students, faculty, staff, and allies. Kickback is a low-stakes event where participants can engage with campus partners and the community.
