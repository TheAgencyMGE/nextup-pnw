CJ 101: What Is a Criminal Justice Degree?

UW Tacoma Events

Curious about criminal justice but not sure what the degree actually covers? Join UW Tacoma’s School of Social Work & Criminal Justice for a virtual introduction to the Criminal Justice major. We’ll explore what criminal justice students study, the kinds of careers a CJ degree can lead to, and what makes UW Tacoma’s program unique. You’ll also learn about our on-campus and online degree options , admission requirements, and next steps for applying. Whether you’re just beginning to explore criminal justice or already considering UW Tacoma, this is a great place to start. Prospective students, transfer students, and supporters are welcome.
