Three Minute Thesis Competition Final Round

Boise State University Events

The Three Minute Thesis (3MT) competition challenges graduate students to communicate their research, creative work, paper, project, thesis or dissertation to a general audience in just three minutes using one slide . The competition helps students develop communication skills to share the impact of their work with the university, our local community and the world. Students will compete for a chance to advance to the Idaho Statewide 3MT competition and win up to 3,000 dollars in prizes. Boise State students, faculty, staff and community members are invited to attend, learn about the exciting work happening across our graduate programs and support our student presenters.
