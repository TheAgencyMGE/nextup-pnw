EOA Virtual Office Hours

Oregon State University Events

The Office of Equal Opportunity and Access (EOA) is offering drop-in virtual office hours during fall term. This informal space is available to OSU faculty, staff, and students who have general questions about EOA services, processes, or resources. EOA staff will be available to answer questions and help connect participants with the appropriate individuals or offices as needed. Details: No registration required OSU authentication is required to join the Zoom meeting Participants will be assisted on a first-come, first-served basis A Zoom waiting room will be used to provide privacy for each participant Whether you have a quick question or are unsure where to start, you're welcome to drop in
