Late Shift Screening and Nursing Industry Panel

UBC Okanagan Events

Explore one of healthcare’s most urgent challenges at this free evening of film and conversation. Watch a screening of Late Shift , a medical drama following a nurse navigating an intense, short-staffed shift on a surgical unit. Afterward, visiting expert Dr. Janie Brown from Australia, whose research focuses on nursing workforce resilience, will join local healthcare leaders and frontline nursing voices from the Okanagan to discuss the film’s themes, including nursing shortages, burnout, workforce retention and the transition from education to practice.
