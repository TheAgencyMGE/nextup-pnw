Experiential Learning with Community Impact (ZOOM)

Boise State University Events

Looking for ways to make learning more applied and engaging for students? Community-engaged projects and service-learning are flexible forms of experiential learning that connect course learning with authentic community needs. In this interactive Zoom workshop, you'll explore examples from a variety of disciplines, learn key design principles, and consider approaches that fit your course goals and capacity. Whether you're making small adjustments or planning a larger course redesign, you'll leave with practical ideas, tools, and support to get started. After participating in this workshop, you will be able to: - Explain how community-engaged experiential learning can support student learning
