InspireME Seminar Series - Daniel Diaz (Univ. of Arizona)

Boise State University Events

Light-material interactions: Physics and applications of laser ablation Light-material interactions (LMI) explore how light couples with matter to drive physicochemical processes. In the thermal regime, laser ablation progresses from heating and melting to plasma formation. This enables precise metal processing, spectroscopic chemical characterization of aerosols and geomaterials, and the shock-wave disruption of supersonic and hypersonic flows. Integrating LMI into STEM curricula provides graduate and undergraduate students with vital engineering insights.
