How Data Addresses Global Trade Complexity

Seattle University Events

Join the MSBA program for an evening of networking, appetizers and a talk by Claudia Loranca Schroeder of IBM. Claudia will talk about how data helps companies manage the complexity of global trade, from product classification and country of origin to customs and trade agreements. She will share lessons from her work in trade compliance and supply chain systems and what this field can mean for MSBA careers. Time for questions will follow. About the speaker Claudia Loranca Schroeder is an Associate Partner at IBM, where she works in Oracle solution architecture.
