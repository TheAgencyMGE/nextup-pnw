Employer On-Campus: Canadian Armed Forces

UBC Career Centre

Major Dugald Thomson will present on his engineering career and research in the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) and a CAF recruiter will provide a brief presentation on STEM-related careers in the CAF. All students interested in learning more about opportunities in the CAF are welcome to attend! Speaker Biography: Major Dugald Thomson is a Canadian Armed Forces officer and underwater acoustics specialist based in Victoria, BC. He joined the CAF in 2000, completed electrical engineering at the Royal Military College of Canada, and served in maritime air operations, including experience flying on the CP-140 Aurora. He later specialized in ocean acoustics, completing a Master’s in Acoustical Oceano
