Whole Woman Wealth: A Conversation

Join the Women of Seattle University for a discussion with AJ Bishop-Andrews, '12 MA, former WofSU Board Member and past Education Committee Chair, on her book Rich Like Her. Whole Woman Wealth is an interactive conversation designed to help women redefine what wealth means in this season of their lives and explore whether the life they’re building reflects what matters most to them. Together, we’ll explore not only financial wealth, but also the wealth of time, relationships, health, emotional well-being, purpose, contribution, and freedom.
