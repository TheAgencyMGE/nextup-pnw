Managing the Ecological Impacts of Shorter and Warmer Winters in the Northwest Deep Dive Webinar

University of Washington Campus Calendar

In this webinar, the NW CASC’s Deep Dive team will present a review of current knowledge and practice around the impacts of shorter and warmer winters on Northwest species and ecosystems. This synthesis is the result of the NW CASC’s 2026 Deep Dive workshop, which convened natural resource managers and scientists from Northwest Tribes, universities, non-profit and private sectors, and federal and state governments to review what is currently known (and unknown) about managing Northwest species and ecosystems in response to a loss of historical winter conditions.
