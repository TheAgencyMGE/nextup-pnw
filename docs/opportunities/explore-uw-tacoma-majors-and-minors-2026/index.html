Explore UW Tacoma: Majors and Minors

UW Tacoma Events

Are you exploring majors, considering a minor, or interested in learning more about our academic programs at UW Tacoma? Join us for Explore UW Tacoma: Majors & Minors on Tuesday, October 27, 2026! This event is a great opportunity to explore academic programs, connect with faculty and students, and learn more about the different majors and minors offered at UW Tacoma. You have three options: Classroom Visits: Experience a live UW Tacoma class with faculty and students. Registration required; class times vary. Majors & Minors Fair: Meet faculty and staff to learn more about academic programs. Stop by anytime between 12:00–2:00 PM in William Philip Hall. No registration required! Speed Major E
