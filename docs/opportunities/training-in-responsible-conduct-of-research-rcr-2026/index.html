Training in Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR)

Oregon State University Events

The College of Liberal Arts and the Office of Research Integrity within the Division of Research and Innovation (DRI) are hosting a nine-month in-person Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR) Training Program on one to two Fridays of each month from 2 p.m. to 3 p.m. in academic year 2026-2027. This seminar series will allow you to complete RCR training requirements in monthly sessions with presentations by OSU faculty and staff.
