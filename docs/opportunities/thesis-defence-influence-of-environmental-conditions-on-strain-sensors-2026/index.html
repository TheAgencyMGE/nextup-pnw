Thesis Defence: Influence of Environmental Conditions on Strain Sensors made from Laser Induced Graphene

UBC Okanagan Events

Tamim Mahmood Tamim, supervised by Dr. Rudolf Seethaler, will defend their thesis titled “Influence of Environmental Conditions on Strain Sensors made from Laser Induced Graphene” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Applied Science in Mechanical Engineering. An abstract for Tamim Mahmood Tamim’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. This examination will be offered in hybrid format. Registration is not required for in-person defences. Please email rudolf.seethaler@ubc.ca to receive the Zoom link for this defence.
