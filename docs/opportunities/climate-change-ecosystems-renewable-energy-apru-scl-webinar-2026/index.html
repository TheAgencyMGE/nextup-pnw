Climate Change, Ecosystems, Renewable Energy APRU SCL Webinar

University of Oregon Events

This month's APRU SCL webinar will explore the role of climate change research and renewable energy in building sustainable cities and landscapes . Hear about remote sensing observations of forest ecosystems, the impacts of climate change on terrestrial carbon and nitrogen cycles, and approaches to developing climate-resilient wind and solar power systems. The event will be livestreamed and is open to researchers, practitioners, students, and policymakers both within and outside the APRU SCL network.
