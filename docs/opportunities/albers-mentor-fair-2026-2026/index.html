Albers Mentor Fair 2026

Seattle University Events

The Albers Mentor Fair is the kick off event for three main mentor programs: Albers Mentor Program for Albers Grad students, and Undergrad Seniors. Albers Accounting Mentor Program for Albers Accounting Grad students and Undergrad Juniors and Seniors SEM MBA Mentor Program available only for students enrolled in the Albers SEM MBA program. At the Albers Mentor Fair, students have a chance to connect with professionals who are volunteering and mentors in the three Albers Mentor Programs. This is a unique opportunity to interact with upper-level executives from leading Puget Sound companies and organizations. Small groups of one to three students meet monthly with a mentor throughout the acade
