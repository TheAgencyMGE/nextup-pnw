Introduction to R

University of Oregon Events

Are you interested in using R and RStudio but not sure where to begin? This eight-session workshop series will introduce the fundamentals of working with R. It’s designed for absolute beginners or anyone who wants to review the basics. Each session is one hour, and will cover the following topics: Intro to R and RStudio Getting Started with R, Part 1 Getting Started with R, Part 2 Data Visualization with R & ggplot2 Functions Explained Working with Data in R, Part 1 Working with Data in R, Part 2 Reproducible Reports with R & R Markdown This series follows the Carpentries workshop curriculum.
