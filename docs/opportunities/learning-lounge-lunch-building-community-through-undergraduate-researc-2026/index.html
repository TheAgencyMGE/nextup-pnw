Learning Lounge @ Lunch: Building Community Through Undergraduate Research in Non-Lab Disciplines

Boise State University Events

Join us for lunch and a conversation with Dr. Rafael P. Ribas (Department of Economics) about why and how to involve undergraduate students in research, particularly in disciplines where research is not traditionally organized around labs. He will discuss approaches designed to build a community around research, support students in pursuing more ambitious career goals, and help them develop the confidence and agency to direct their own learning and work. Participants will also have an opportunity to brainstorm how one or more of these approaches could be adapted to their own teaching.
