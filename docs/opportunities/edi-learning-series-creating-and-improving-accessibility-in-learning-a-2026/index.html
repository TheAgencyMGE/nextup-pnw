EDI Learning Series: Creating and Improving Accessibility in Learning and Working Spaces on Campus

UBC Okanagan Events

This interactive workshop will provide a space for participants to engage in a conversation about disability and its impacts, and to explore how disability might be brought into the conversations concerning the roles that staff and faculty have on campus. Hear from presenters with lived experience and explore tools to create and improve accessibility. This session is led by the Disability Resource Centre and the Centre for Teaching and Learning, in partnership with the Equity and Inclusion Office. Registration is limited. Register here Light refreshments will be provided.
