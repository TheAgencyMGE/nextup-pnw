Thesis Defence: ‘A Person is a Person through other Persons’

UBC Okanagan Events

Bukunmi Mary Okoduwa, supervised by Dr. Madeleine Ransom, will defend their thesis titled “‘A Person is a Person through other Persons’: Developing an Ubuntu Account of Trustworthiness for Global Trustworthy AI” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Arts in Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies – Global Studies Theme. An abstract for Bukunmi Mary Okoduwa’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public.
