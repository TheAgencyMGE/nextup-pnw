Morley Nelson Snake River Birds of Prey National Conservation Area Symposium

Boise State University Events

The NCA Symposium is a two-day symposium of talks, poster presentations, and collaboration focused on the Morley Nelson Snake River Birds of Prey National Conservation Area (NCA). It showcases research, conservation-based projects, and partnerships associated with the NCA to facilitate interactions and research development with university students, local agencies, NGOs, private corporations, and the general public. Registration deadlines: Oct. 30, 2026, for presenters and Nov. 10, 2026, for other attendees.
