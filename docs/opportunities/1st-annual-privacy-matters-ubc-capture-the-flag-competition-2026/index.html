Privacy Matters @ UBC

The 1st Annual Privacy Matters @ UBC Capture the Flag Competition is kicking off on October 1st and running throughout the month. Whether you want to go head-to-head solo or rally your crew and compete as a team, there’s a spot for you on the leaderboard (and bragging rights are very much on the table!). The format is straightforward: solve a series of progressive cybersecurity challenges, each one unlocking the next. New challenges drop throughout the month, so you’ll want to check back often and keep an eye on where you and your rivals stack up. There are hundreds of dollars in prizes up for grabs, including awards that go beyond just finishing first.
