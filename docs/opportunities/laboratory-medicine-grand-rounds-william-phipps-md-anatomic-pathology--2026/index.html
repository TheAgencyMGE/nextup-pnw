Combined DLMP Grand Rounds: William Phipps, MD - Anatomic Pathology Mass Spectrometry Updates

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Combined DLMP Grand Rounds: All members from Laboratory Medicine & Pathology are encouraged to participate in this seminar with applications on both sides of the department. (Part of the Laboratory Medicine Grand Rounds series.) William Phipps, MD Assistant Professor, Chemistry Director, Tissue Mass Spectrometry Assistant Director, Clinical Chemistry Department of Laboratory Medicine & Pathology University of Washington Objectives: Discern role of liquid chromatography mass spectrometry as a complement to IHC at UW. Discover multiple mass spectrometry acquisition modes improving our practice. See novel developments in the application of LCMS to the diagnosis of Hirschsprung disease. Speaker
