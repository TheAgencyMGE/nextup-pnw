CFES Group Fitness Training Course

UBC Okanagan Events

Would you like to become a group fitness instructor? Well, now is your chance. Join Maria as she teaches the practical skills and professional ethics required to be a successful group fitness instructor. Prerequisite: Completion of the first year of Human Kinetics, a 100-level Human Anatomy course, or the Canadian Fitness Education Services (CFES) Fitness Knowledge Course. This course is open to all UBCO students, faculty, staff and community members with the required prerequisite.
