Learning Lounge @ Lunch: Creating Immersive Learning Experiences in The Luminary (HYBRID)

Boise State University Events

Join us for lunch and to talk with Dr. Beret Norman (Department of World Languages) and Garrett Ryan (Programs & Projects Coordinator, School of the Arts) about how Dr. Norman created a summative assessment experience for her students using The Stein Luminary. Participants will also hear other examples of how the Luminary can be used to create immersive learning environments, and have a chance to brainstorm how they could take advantage of this innovative campus resource. A selection of sandwiches and salads will be available for those attending this session in person. We will have dairy and gluten free options available, as well as vegetarian/vegan food.
