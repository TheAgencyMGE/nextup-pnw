Wild Rockies Field Institute Table/Information Session

Oregon State University Events

The Wild Rockies Field Institute is an off-campus study opportunity. WRFI offers academic field-based courses through the University of Montana. Our courses take place in the “Wild Rockies” of North America and combine rigorous academic inquiry with cultural immersion and extended backcountry expeditions. Students join us from colleges and universities across North America and from a wide variety of majors. We currently offer courses in Environmental Studies, Natural Resources & Science Management, Geography and Philosophy.
