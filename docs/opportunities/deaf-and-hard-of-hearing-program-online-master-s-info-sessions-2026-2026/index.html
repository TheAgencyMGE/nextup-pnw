Deaf and Hard of Hearing Program Online Master’s Info Sessions 2026

ECPS Department, Faculty of Education

Interested in becoming a teacher of the Deaf and Hard of Hearing? Join us for an online information session to learn more about UBC’s two-year Online Master’s program, including its cohort model, practicum component, and Capstone Inquiry Project. ASL interpreting and captions will be provided. Information sessions will be held on September 23, October 29, and November 17 from 4:30–5:30 p.m. PST, and you are welcome to drop in at any time.
