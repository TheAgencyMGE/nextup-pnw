Learn By Doing: The Geology of Common Everyday Products

Oregon State University Events

Learn by Doing is your opportunity to get out into campus, nature or the community, get to know other new students, and learn while you’re doing it! No matter which Learn by Doing experience you choose, you'll make memories and build new connections to Beaver Nation. Faculty and students from the GeoClub in the College of Earth, Ocean, and Atmospheric Sciences (CEOAS) will set up an interactive display at Wilkinson Hall with samples of naturally occurring geological materials in their typical geological occurrence and in their refined form.
