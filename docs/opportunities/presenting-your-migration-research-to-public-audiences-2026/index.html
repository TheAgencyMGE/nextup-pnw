Presenting Your Migration Research to Public Audiences

Centre for Migration Studies

How do you explain years of research to someone outside your field in just a few minutes? This workshop is designed to help those studying migration, mobility, and displacement communicate their research clearly and effectively to interdisciplinary and public audiences. Graduate students and postdoctoral research fellows are especially welcome, particularly those selected to present at the New Insights on Migration and Mobilities: Graduate Research Showcase on December 9. Participants will learn strategies for crafting impactful presentations, engaging diverse audiences, and designing compelling slides. The 60-minute workshop will be followed by an optional 30-minute practice session.
