Mission V.I.B.E.S.: The process of sending a science payload to space Aged individuals and astronauts experience bone loss despite rigorous physical activity. Bone mechanoresponse is partly regulated by mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) that respond to mechanical challenges. To investigate if microgravity affects the mechanoresponse of MSCs in bone, we developed 3D Bone Marrow Analogs (BMAs) to be maintained in hardware aboard the International Space Station (ISS). To create a BMA model, we generated two idealized 3D trabecular lattices mimicking young (25%) or old (13%) trabecular bone volumes and filled them with hydrogel-encapsulated primary bone marrow MSCs to mimic bone marrow.
