As the World Burns: What does 21st-century wildfire resilience look like?

UBC Okanagan Events

About the event What if we could reduce the devastating impacts of wildfire before the next fire season begins? As wildfires become more frequent and severe, communities around the world are facing urgent questions about how to prepare for an increasingly fire-prone future. We often hear about the need for “wildfire resilience,” but what does that look like in practice? Drawing on the latest research and real-world examples, this talk explores how science can help us better understand wildfire risk and identify effective ways to reduce it.
