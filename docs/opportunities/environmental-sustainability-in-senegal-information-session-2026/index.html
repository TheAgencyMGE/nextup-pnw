Environmental Sustainability in Senegal Information Session

University of Oregon Events

Interested in exploring environmental issues and community resilience in West Africa? Join the information session for Environmental Health and Community Resilience in Senegal to learn about this four-week study abroad program. Discover how local communities are addressing climate change, environmental health, and sustainable development through immersive coursework, hands-on projects, and guided excursions. Students will also have the opportunity to learn basic Wolof language skills and engage with local communities. This information session goes over all of the program details and requirements.
