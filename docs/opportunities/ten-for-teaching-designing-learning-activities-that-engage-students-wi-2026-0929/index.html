Ten for Teaching: Designing learning activities that engage students with one another and course content (ASYNCHRONOUS)

Boise State University Events

Active learning includes students in the learning process through activities that engage them with the course content (and often with one another). In this workshop, we will consider the benefits to students from engaging in active learning and explore a variety of methods and formats which can be combined to form engaging activities which align with class learning outcomes. We will also talk about activities that can be used to open and close class sessions in ways that reinforce learning. Finally, you will have a chance to begin building a lesson plan that considers students’ prior knowledge and incorporates active learning.
