Title: Seasonal Variability Emerges as a Driver of Stream Biodiversity Program: Biology MS Committee Chair: Jodi Brandt Committee: Jodi Brandt, Trevor Caughlin, Jen Cruz, Charles Hawkins Abstract: Humans have dramatically changed seasonal variability across many ecosystems. Seasonal variability may increase biodiversity by generating heterogeneous habitats, yet also reduce aquatic diversity by intensifying water limitation and habitat contraction. Using nine years of seasonal remote-sensing data paired with 1,718 stream surveys, we tested whether macroinvertebrate biodiversity in drylands responds more strongly to water availability or seasonal variability.
