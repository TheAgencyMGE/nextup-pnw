Knight Campus Undergraduate Scholars Program Info Session for CHC Students

University of Oregon Events

Join representatives from the Clark Honors College and the UO’s Knight Campus for Accelerating Scientific Impact to learn about the Knight Campus Undergraduate Scholars Program. Through this program, students with sophomore or junior standing who are majoring in a natural science field are offered a mentored research opportunity (along with $16,000 of funding!) in a Knight Campus lab in 2027. Attend this Zoom info session to learn more about how to apply!
