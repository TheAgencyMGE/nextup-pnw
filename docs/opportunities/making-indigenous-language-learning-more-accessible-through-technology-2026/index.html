Making Indigenous Language Learning More Accessible Through Technology with K’odi Taylor

UBC Learning Circle

What happens when an Indigenous language learner with ADHD sets out to create the resources he wished he’d had as a child? Join K’odi Taylor, founder of First-ory, as he shares his personal language learning journey and the work he’s doing as a resource creator. Drawing on lived experience, research, and years of exploring what makes learning effective and engaging, K’odi will discuss barriers learners face and the opportunities technology creates to support them.
