SparkShop: Yes You Can - Active Learning in Large Classes (ZOOM)

Boise State University Events

Sparkshops are short, 25-minute interactive Zoom sessions focused on high-demand teaching topics. This SparkShop focuses on strategies for actively engaging students in their learning, particularly in large courses. By the end of the session, participants will be able to list active learning strategies applicable for their course size setting. --- Event Details: https://boisestate.campusgroups.com/rsvp?id=397186
