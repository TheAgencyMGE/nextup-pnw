Mindfulness in Medicine: A Workshop for Healthcare Providers

Oregon State University Events

Co-hosted by Marigold and Samaritan Health Services Led by Rebekah Byrne, MD MPH Are you a physician or an advanced practice provider (including nurse practitioners, physician's assistants, certified nurse midwives, and certified registered nurse anesthetists) who is seeking to restore connection with meaning and purpose in your work? Join for an immersive, retreat-like workshop focused on exploring mindfulness in medicine through experiential programming, including formal and informal mindfulness practices, narrative medicine, and time spent in building community.
