Applying an Implementation Science Lens to Extreme Heat Interventions: Challenges and Ways Forward

University of Washington Campus Calendar

The University of Washington REACH Center is hosting the next session of its Implementation Science in Extreme Weather and Health Webinar Series on Tuesday, October 27 from 10-11 am Pacific Time. Jeremy Hess, MD, MPH, of the University of Washington, will present his team’s work on using implementation science to assess intervention effectiveness and to evaluate a decision support tool delivery platform for local health departments. The webinars are designed for health practitioners and researchers interested in implementation science and extreme weather and health adaptation. All are welcome! Register below:
