Pedagogies of Play

Green College

This first workshop in The Critical Play Speakers’ Series focuses on how the interactive, dynamic experiences at the heart of games can be used as new kind of teaching experience. Christopher Kawika Guillermo (Dr Christopher B Patterson) of UBC’s Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice, and Vancouver-based game designer and programmer Tim Bellefleur from Chronograph Games, reveal their pedagogies of play, and how the unexpected, emergent properties of games create new opportunities for students (and players) to teach themselves and others. Co-organized with the new Critical Play Lab , part of UBC’s Pop Culture Cluster ; co-sponsored by UBC’s Public Humanities Hub . Tim Belle
