Doctoral Writing Circle

Oregon State University Events

This is an online circle for any OSU student in a doctoral program of study who wants dedicated time to work quietly or get feedback on any writing they are doing. Contact Adam Haley at haleyad@oregonstate.edu to join. The Graduate Writing Center offers writing circles for accountability, feedback, and professional development purposes to graduate students and post-doctorates. These small groups meet weekly online or in person with a GWC Consultant to discuss work they are doing, get feedback on that work, and write silently together.
