Action for Climate Team Info Session

UBC Okanagan Events

Interested in climate action and sustainability? Join us at the Sawchuk Family Theatre between 11 am and 2 pm, on October 1 to learn more about the Action for Climate Team (ACT) and sustainable initiatives on campus. There will be sustainability-themed trivia, arts and crafts and snacks as we chat about what the projects are going to be and how to get involved this year. This is a drop-in event. Feel free to come and go as you need to. There may be a moderate level of noise as people participate in trivia, as well as music in the background.
