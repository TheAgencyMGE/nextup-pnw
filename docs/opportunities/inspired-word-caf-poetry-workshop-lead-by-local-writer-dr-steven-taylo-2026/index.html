Inspired Word Café: Poetry workshop lead by local writer Dr. Steven Taylor

UBC Okanagan Events

In collaboration with the Okanagan Regional Library (Mission Branch), Inspired Word Café is thrilled to present a poetry workshop led by local writer Dr. Steven Taylor! This workshop invites writers at all stages and experience levels to engage in a learning and sharing environment. No registration necessary—spots are first come, first served. This event is free to attend. About the workshop This workshop will feature accessible and welcoming writing exercises for all skill levels, with opportunities to share your work and receive feedback from Dr. Taylor and fellow group members on your in-session writing. Each of the two sessions stands alone, with a different focus for each workshop. Work
