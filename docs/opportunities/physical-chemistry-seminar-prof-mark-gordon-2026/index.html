Physical Chemistry Seminar: Prof. Mark Gordon

University of Washington Campus Calendar

"The Effective Fragment Potential: A Gateway to Chemical Insight and High-Performance Computing" Professor Mark Gordon - Department of Chemistry, Iowa State University Host: Anne McCoy The effective fragment potential (EFP) is an ab initio force field that is derived entirely from quantum mechanics with no empirically fitted parameters. Intermolecular interactions predicted by the EFP method are comparable in accuracy to that of second order perturbation theory (MP2) and close to that of coupled cluster (CC) theory.
