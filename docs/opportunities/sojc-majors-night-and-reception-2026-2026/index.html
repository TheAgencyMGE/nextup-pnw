SOJC Majors Night and Reception 2026

University of Oregon Events

You're invited to the School of Journalism and Communication 2026 Majors Night and Reception! Learn about our four undergraduate majors, four minors, experiential learning and professional development opportunities, careers, and more! If you're a new SOJC major, existing SOJC major, pursuing an SOJC minor, or curious about a career as a professional or creative communicator, this event is right for you! This event isn't just for first-year students and transfer students—sophomores, juniors, and seniors can all benefit and discover possibilities they didn't know of and new avenues for career exploration within their major.
