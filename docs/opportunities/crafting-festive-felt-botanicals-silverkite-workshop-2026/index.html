Crafting: Festive Felt Botanicals - SilverKite Workshop

Sno-Isle Libraries

Join us as SilverKite artist Cassie Mira shows us how to make a brooch or seasonal decor inspired by leaves and flowers. Using felt fabric participants will cut, layer and shape to create crafted botanicals. These creations can be worn on a lapel or blouse or made to adorn your table. Embellish with thread and/or beads. This workshop offers options to glue and/or stitch and includes pins for making the project wearable.
