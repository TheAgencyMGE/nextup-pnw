Scholarship Writing Workshop Series: Division of Graduate Studies Awards Ready to strengthen your scholarship application? Join us for a two-part workshop series designed to help you write a competitive Division of Graduate Studies Scholarship application. Light refreshments provided. RSVP: https://tinyurl.com/DGSScholarship2027 Open to all current UO graduate students.
