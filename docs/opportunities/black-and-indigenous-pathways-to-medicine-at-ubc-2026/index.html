Black and Indigenous Pathways to Medicine at UBC

UBC Okanagan Events

Join the Faculty of Medicine’s Black and Indigenous Initiatives Coordinators to learn about UBC’s Medical Doctor Undergraduate Program admissions process and the respective pathways to a career in medicine. The event is in-person only and free to attend. Registration is recommended. Register to attend For more information, contact Jackson Traplin at jackson.traplin@ubc.ca or Felix Amoh-Siaw at felix.amohsiaw@ubc.ca
