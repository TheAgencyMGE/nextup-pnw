Ecampus Research Fellows Program information session

Oregon State University Events

If you’re interested in conducting research about online teaching and learning, the Ecampus Research Unit will help you plan your proposal for the Ecampus Research Fellows Program. The Research Unit annually funds up to three faculty and/or department projects — $25,000 each — to support research on distance and online education. Info sessions will be held Tuesday, Sept. 29 at 2 p.m. and Friday, Oct.2 at 10 a.m. via Zoom. Applications are due Nov. 1. To register for one of the info sessions or ask a question, contact Naomi Aguiar ( naomi.aguiar@oregonstate.edu ), associate director of research.
