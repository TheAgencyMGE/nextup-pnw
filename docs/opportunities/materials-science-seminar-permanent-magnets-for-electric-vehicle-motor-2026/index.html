Materials Science Seminar: Permanent Magnets for Electric Vehicle Motors: Rare-Earth Reduction and Alternative Material Strategies

Boise State University Events

Abstract: The rapid expansion of the electric vehicle market is driving unprecedented demand for high-power-density traction motors that rely on advanced permanent magnets. Sintered Nd-Fe-B magnets currently dominate this application due to their exceptional energy product and thermal stability. However, surging rare-earth demand compounded by ongoing geopolitical tensions has created critical supply-chain vulnerabilities, particularly for heavy rare-earth elements such as dysprosium and terbium.
