UW Campus Deadline for DAAD Study Scholarships & Research Grants

UW Bothell Events

Would you like to study, carry out research or learn German in Germany and are you looking for funding? Every year, the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) supports well over 100,000 German and international students and researchers around the globe – making it the world's largest funding organisation of its kind. DAAD scholarships are aimed primarily at bachelor's graduates, doctoral students and postdocs and are awarded for study and research visits to universities and non-university research institutions in Germany.
