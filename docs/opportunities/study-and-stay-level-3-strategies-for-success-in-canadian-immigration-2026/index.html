Study and Stay Level 3 — Strategies for Success in Canadian Immigration

UBC Okanagan Events

Planning to stay in Canada after graduation? Our Study and Stay Permanent Residence workshops are designed to help you navigate the immigration pathways, policies, and processes you’ll encounter when applying for Permanent Residence. This Level 3 Study & Stay session ill guide you in aligning your career planning with Canada’s immigration priorities to maximize your competitive advantage. You will gain essential insights into the National Occupational Classification (NOC) system and practical tips to boost your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score, paving the way from international student to permanent resident. Don’t miss this chance to stand out and take charge of your future in Canada
