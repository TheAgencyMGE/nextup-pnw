Panhellenic Primary Recruitment Orientation

Oregon State University Events

Pre‑registration is required. Recruitment events will take place Thursday, Sept. 24 through Tuesday, Sept. 29. Panhellenic is the governing council for 11 housed sororities , each offering strong sisterhood, leadership opportunities, academic support, and a vibrant community. With an average chapter size of around 100 members , joining a Panhellenic sorority is one of the best ways to make the most of your Oregon State experience.
