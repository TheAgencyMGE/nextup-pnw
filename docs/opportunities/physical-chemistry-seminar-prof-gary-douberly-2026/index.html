Physical Chemistry Seminar: Prof. Gary Douberly

University of Washington Campus Calendar

"Spectroscopy of Molecular Aggregates in Helium Droplets." Professor Gary E. Douberly - Department of Chemistry, Washington State University Host: Anne McCoy The first beam of helium droplets was reported in the 1961 paper Strahlen aus kondensiertem Helium im Hochvakuum by Von E. W. Becker and co-workers [1]. However, molecular spectroscopy of helium-solvated dopants wasn't realized until 30 years later in the laboratories of Scoles and Toennies [2,3]. It has now been over three decades since this early, seminal work on doped helium droplets, yet the field of helium droplet spectroscopy remains fresh with significant potential for addressing a diverse set of problems in molecular science.
