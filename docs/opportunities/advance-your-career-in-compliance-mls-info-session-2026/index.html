Advance Your Career in Compliance: MLS Info Session

Seattle University Events

Join us for a live online information session to learn more about Seattle University School of Law's Online Master of Legal Studies (MLS) in Compliance and Risk Management. This flexible, part-time program is designed for professionals who work in highly regulated industries and want to deepen their legal knowledge, without becoming a lawyer. During this session, you'll: Get an overview of the program structure and fully online format Learn about available concentrations (including Healthcare, Sports, Financial, Cybersecurity & Privacy, and Corporate Compliance) Explore career paths for MLS graduates Understand admissions requirements and upcoming deadlines Ask your questions live!
