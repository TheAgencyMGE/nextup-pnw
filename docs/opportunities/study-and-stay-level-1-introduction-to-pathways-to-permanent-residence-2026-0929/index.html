Study and Stay Level 1 — Introduction to Pathways to Permanent Residence

UBC Okanagan Events

Planning to stay in Canada after graduation? Our Study and Stay Permanent Residence workshops are designed to help you navigate the immigration pathways, policies, and processes you’ll encounter when applying for Permanent Residence. In this session, you will begin to position yourself within the context of Canadian immigration and map your Study and Stay learning to your immigration goals. This introductory session is Level 1 in the Study and Stay series and provides an overview of the BC Provincial Nominee Program (BC PNP) Skills Immigration and Express Entry immigration streams, which are most commonly used by international students and alumni.
