Vanpool Info Session

University of Oregon Events

Are you curious about the benefits of vanpooling? Please join UO Transportation Services and Lane Transit District in Teams for a virtual vanpool info session. Learn about the program from transportation specialists who will answer all your questions. LTD’s Employer Program provides a subsidy of up to $675 per month to each vanpool. Vanpool is a great alternative to solo driving for many reasons: Save money on the expense of your commute. Put less wear and tear on your personal vehicle. Enjoy the company of others while you travel. Relax while you ride, instead of always having to drive. For more information on vanpooling, please visit our website at www.ltd.org/vanpool .
