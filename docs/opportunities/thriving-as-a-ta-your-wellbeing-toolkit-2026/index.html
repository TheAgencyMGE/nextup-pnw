Thriving as a TA: Your Wellbeing Toolkit

UBC Okanagan Events

TAing can be one of the most rewarding parts of your university experience, and looking after your own wellbeing helps keep it that way. Being a TA means wearing a lot of hats, including student, teacher, employee and more. That balancing act has the potential to take its toll. This interactive, in-person workshop gives you a head start on your wellbeing before the term ramps up. You’ll learn to recognize the early signs of stress and burnout, discover campus supports available to you as both a student and an employee, and leave with a personal action plan of realistic strategies for your own wellbeing.
