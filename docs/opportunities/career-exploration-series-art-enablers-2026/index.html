Career Exploration Series: Art Enablers

UBC Okanagan Events

Ready to turn your creative passion into a career? Attend this casual networking event to get a look behind the scenes of the arts and entertainment industry. Whether you are building your path in music, visual arts, film, or written media, this event cuts through the noise to give you direct access to the executives and facilitators shaping today’s cultural landscape. Learn exactly what it takes to thrive in the industry from the people who are already doing it. Real talk with real professionals making a real difference in the art industry. Open to all UBCO students and UBC alumni. Guests: Acadia Bromke – Editor-in-Chief and Publicist, Braze Magazine Cruz Bebe – Studio Operations Director,
