Veteran and Military Affiliated Welcome

University of Oregon Events

The Veteran Student Welcome and Information Session is recommended for all incoming first years, graduate students, current student veterans, transfers, and family seeking information about veteran and military connected student resources on campus. You may identify as a military connected student if you are active duty, veteran, guard, reserve, or a family member. We will provide a free lunch! This session will provide information about resources, helpful specifically to military connected students, and include ways to connect and network on campus, as well as opportunity to meet other new and returning military connected students.
