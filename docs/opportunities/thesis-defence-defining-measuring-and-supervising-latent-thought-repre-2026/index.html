Thesis Defence: Defining, Measuring, and Supervising Latent Thought Representations in Large Language Models

UBC Okanagan Events

Fahd Seddik, supervised by Dr. Fatemeh Fard, will defend their thesis titled “Defining, Measuring, and Supervising Latent Thought Representations in Large Language Models” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Science in Computer Science. An abstract and overview for Fahd Seddik’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person defences.
