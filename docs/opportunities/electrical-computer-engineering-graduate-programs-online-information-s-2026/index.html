Electrical & Computer Engineering Graduate Programs Online Information Session

UW Bothell Events

Prepare to become an innovator and researcher in electrical and computer engineering. Electrical and computer engineers innovate and improve the devices and systems we use every day. From mobile phones and medical devices to renewable energy, society needs technically competent professionals who apply ethical standards and best practices that reflect the importance of societal and global needs. Join us for an online information session featuring the School of STEM Electrical & Computer Engineering graduate programs, featuring an in-depth overview of the Master of Science in Electrical & Computer Engineering and Graduate Certificate in Electrical & Computer Engineering Foundations .
