Inclusive Teaching: Introduction to Universal Design for Learning Session Format:

Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology

This is an introductory interactive online workshop on Universal Design for Learning (UDL) to discover the essentials of creating inclusive learning environments. This session will guide you through the roots, principles, and practical applications of UDL, offering insights into how to accommodate diverse learning needs in educational settings. Engage with educators and experts in an enriching experience, complete with real-world examples, hands-on activities, and a few resources for implementing UDL strategies in your practice.
