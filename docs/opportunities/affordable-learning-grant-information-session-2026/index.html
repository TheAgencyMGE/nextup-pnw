Affordable Learning Grant Information Session

Oregon State University Events

Faculty who are interested in applying for an Affordable Leaning Grant or are just curious about open educational resources and how using these can help lower the cost of course materials for students are invited to join in a webinar to learn more about OER, the grants, and the services offered by the Open Educational Resources Unit . All faculty regardless of teaching modality are welcome to join.
