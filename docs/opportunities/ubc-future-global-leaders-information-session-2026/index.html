UBC Future Global Leaders – Information Session

UBC Extended Learning

UBC Future Global Leaders is an exciting pre-university program for high school students ages 15-18. If you are a high school student thinking about attending a pre-university program this summer, or a parent thinking about the pros and cons of sending your teen, then we encourage you to attend this information session. You will have the chance to meet members of the Future Global Leaders team and learn more about the program, including the courses, instructors, schedule, social activities and more.
