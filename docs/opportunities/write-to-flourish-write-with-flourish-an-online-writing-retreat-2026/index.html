Write to Flourish, Write with Flourish: An Online Writing Retreat

Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication

By popular demand, the Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication is excited to launch a new online writing retreat , open to all members of the UBC community. Whether you’re working on a dissertation, research article, proposal, course paper, or any other project, join us online for dedicated, distraction-free writing time in community with others. Structured writing sessions will help you stay focused, build momentum, and make meaningful progress on your work from wherever you choose to write. Facilitator: Centre for Writing and Scholarly Communication Location: Zoom (link sent one day before)
