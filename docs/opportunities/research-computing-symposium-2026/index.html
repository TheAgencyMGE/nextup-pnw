Research Computing Symposium

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Join us for our inaugural Research Computing Symposium, an event bringing together the UW research community to showcase current work, explore emerging capabilities, and connect around the future of research computing. The event will feature a research showcase with lightning talks from UW researchers, highlighting how advanced computing resources and services are supporting research across disciplines. Attendees will also hear about evolving capabilities across our on-premises computing clusters, cloud computing, and related research infrastructure and services.
