Thesis Defence: The establishment of in vitro system in Ochrosia elliptica to improve the accessibility of bioactive ellipticine

UBC Okanagan Events

An abstract for Reo Zukoshi ‘s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person defences.
