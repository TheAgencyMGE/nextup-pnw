Botanical Fiber Art Workshop: Magical Mushroom Embroidery Basics (in-person)

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Class Cost: $50, pre-registration required REGISTER ONLINE Limited financial aid slots offered on a first-come, first-serve basis. Let’s stitch magical mushrooms! Learn to embroider a playful bunch of fungi in this embroidery workshop with fiber artist Melissa Galbraith of MCreativeJ. This two-hour, hands-on workshop covers a variety of beginner-friendly embroidery stitches such as the satin stitch, back stitch, reverse chain stitch, long and short satin stitch, and French knot.
