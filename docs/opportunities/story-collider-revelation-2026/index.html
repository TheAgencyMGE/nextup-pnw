Story Collider: Revelation

Boise State University Events

The Story Collider is delighted to partner with Boise State University to share true, personal science stories from their graduate engineering students. Join us for an evening of stories about revelations—the moments that change everything. From unexpected diagnoses and career-defining detours to finding the courage to speak up, discovering where you belong, and realizing you're capable of more than you imagined, these stories explore how science and engineering can illuminate not only the world around us, but ourselves. Come for the science, stay for the heart. Doors open at 6:00 p.m. MT. Show starts at 6:30 p.m. MT. Hosted by Bryan Kett and Meisa Salaita.
