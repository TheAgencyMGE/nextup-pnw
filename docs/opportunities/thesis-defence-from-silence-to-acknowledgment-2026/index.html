Thesis Defence: From Silence to Acknowledgment

UBC Okanagan Events

Chinedum Agwu, supervised by Dr. David Jefferess, will defend their thesis titled “From Silence to Acknowledgment: Integrating the Igbo Perspective of the 1967-1970 Nigeria-Biafra War Into the Post-Conflict Nigerian History Education Curriculum” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Arts in English. An abstract for Chinedum Agwu’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person defences.
