Applying to Graduate School at UBC Okanagan

UBC Okanagan Events

Thinking of applying to graduate school? In this session, we will break down the graduate school application process at UBC Okanagan (UBCO), including admission requirements and key application elements. College of Graduate Studies staff will also provide tips on building a strong grad school application. This session will cover: Why UBCO? The application process Identifying potential supervisors Understanding admission requirements Developing your application components Funding your education Using the online application The process after submitting an application The session will end with breakout rooms with faculty and staff who can answer your program-specific questions.
