Legal Studies in Wales/Legal Studies in Lillie Info Session

University of Oregon Events

Are you in the legal studies field and want to go abroad? Attend this info session and learn more about how you can apply and expand your knowlege in legal studies abroad. This info session will go over two programs, Legal Studies in Wales and Legal Studies in Lillie. This event is part of International Education Month (IEM) programming. View the full calendar of IEM events: https://international.uoregon.edu/IEM .
