Postdoctoral Fellows Benefits and Payroll Information Session

UBC Okanagan Events

UBCO Human Resources and Payroll are hosting an in-person information session designed specifically for postdoctoral appointees. Join us on Thursday, November 12, from 11 am to noon to learn about your UBC benefits, MSP and how to view your payslip and T4, as well as other need-to-know Payroll tasks. The session includes a short presentation followed by time for your questions. Open to all new and continuing postdocs who are eligible for benefits. Register here
