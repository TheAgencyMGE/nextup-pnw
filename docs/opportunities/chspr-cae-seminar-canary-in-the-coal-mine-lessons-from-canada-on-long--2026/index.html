CHSPR/CAE Seminar | Canary in the Coal Mine – Lessons from Canada on Long Stay Patients

School of Population and Public Health

Speaker: Jason Sutherland, UBC Centre for Health Services and Policy Research Jason Sutherland will examine the Canadian health system’s experience with long-stay hospital patients and how these cases reflect system pressures and affect funding and patient outcomes. Hospitals have faced challenges with Alternative Level of Care patients for decades, and the problems are largely unaddressed. This presentation was originally given in August 2026 at the Evolution of the Care Economy Summit in Sydney, Australia. Jason Sutherland is the UBC Professor in Health Services and Policy and the Director of the Centre for Health Services and Policy Research at the UBC School of Population and Public Heal
