Innovation Lab Makerspace LED Workshop

Seattle University Events

Light Up the Engineer in You! Join the College of Science & Engineering and the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering for an engaging, hands-on creativity workshop during Homecoming & Family Weekend. Guided by faculty and experienced student makers, you will learn the basics of LEDs and circuits while building your very own personalized Rudy the Redhawk LED board to take home as a keepsake. No prior engineering experience is required. All are welcome! Questions? Please contact Kayla Okonu at kokonu@seattleu.edu or 206.220.8228
