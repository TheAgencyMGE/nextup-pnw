Tour of the Seattle Housing Authority Yesler Terrace Housing Development

Join us for a walking tour the Yesler Terrace Development project, and hear from a panel of residents, Seattle Housing Authority staff, and Seattle University representatives about the revitalization project that began in 2013, the lived experiences of the residents, and ways in which Seattle University has partnered with Seattle Housing Authority to support the Yesler Terrace community. The Yesler Terrace Development is a 30-acre site near downtown Seattle, just up the street from Pioneer Square and next to Seattle’s Chinatown-International District.
