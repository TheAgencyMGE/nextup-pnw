HMSC Research Seminar: Toward a deeper understanding of human connections with ocean environments

Oregon State University Events

Speaker: Miriah M. Russo Kelly, Associate Professor, Department of the Environment, Geography & Marine Sciences , Southern Connecticut State University Topic: The Ocean Identity Initiative is a collaborative research agenda developed in response to calls within the UN Ocean Decade to “restore society’s relationship with the ocean” and strengthen society-ocean connections. In response to this global priority, and in consideration of ocean program needs, the Initiative advances Ocean Identity (OI) as a novel construct for examining how individuals engage with, experience, and understand ocean environments through the lens of human identity.
