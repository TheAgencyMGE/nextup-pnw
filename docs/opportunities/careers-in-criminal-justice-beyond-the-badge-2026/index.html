Careers in Criminal Justice: Beyond the Badge

UW Tacoma Events

What can you do with a criminal justice degree? More than you might think! Join UW Tacoma’s School of Social Work & Criminal Justice to explore career paths in victim advocacy, youth services, reentry, restorative justice, policy, and research. Learn what these professionals do, discover ways to build relevant skills and experience, and explore how UW Tacoma’s criminal justice programs can support your goals. Bring your questions and curiosity—whether you’re considering a major, planning to transfer, or exploring a career change.
