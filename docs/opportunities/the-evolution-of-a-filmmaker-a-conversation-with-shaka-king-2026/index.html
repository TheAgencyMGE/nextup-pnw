The Evolution of a Filmmaker: A Conversation with Shaka King

Boise State University Events

Please join the Film and Television Arts Program for a multi-media interview with six-time Oscar-nominated director, screenwriter and producer Shaka King. In conversation with film professor Jim Wareck, King will discuss his journey in the film industry and his evolution as an artist. The discussion will be followed by a Q&A. Event Details Wednesday, October 21, 2026 5 PM The Hemingway Center - 1819 Cesar Chavez Lane, Boise, ID 83725 Free and open to the public About Shaka King Shaka King is a filmmaker with a body of work that spans the film and television arenas.
