Local Plants for Natural Dyes and Pigments (in-person)

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Cost: $65, Advance registration required Financial aid slots available on a first-come, first-served basis REGISTER ONLINE This workshop will provide an introduction to the world of natural dyes with a focus on the use of common garden plants, local ornamental trees and flowers, and even weeds! Students will learn how to prepare dye baths, how different fibers react to natural dyes, and be introduced to the use of mordants. Each student will leave with a swatch book and a silk scarf that they dye themselves. Anneke Wilder is an artist and illustrator based in Seattle, WA.
