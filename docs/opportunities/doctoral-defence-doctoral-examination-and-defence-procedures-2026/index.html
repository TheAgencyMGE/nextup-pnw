Doctoral Defence: Doctoral Examination and Defence Procedures

Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies

The Doctoral Defence information session walks students through the doctoral examination process and explains the different defence modes – in-person, virtual, and hybrid. The session includes: – Steps in the doctoral examination, – Timelines and pressure points, and – Q&A. Facilitator Robyn Starkey is the Doctoral Exams and Thesis Manager with Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies. Robyn’s work is focused on assisting UBC doctoral candidates with preparing for their final examinations. Registration Information Registration is open to current UBC-V graduate students enrolled in a doctoral program.
