Effective Program Assessment: Identifying How Your Program Learning Outcomes Support University Learning Outcomes (ZOOM)

Boise State University Events

"The University Learning Outcomes (ULOs) outline skills every undergraduate student should be developing throughout their time at Boise State, regardless of their major. This year, the PAR Curriculum Map Template has been updated to better capture how programs are supporting the ULOs throughout a student’s journey to their degree. In this workshop, we will review the revised Curriculum Map Template, the ULO/Program Learning Outcome (PLO) alignment guidance provided by the General Education Committee, and discuss ways to make visible how your PLOs support student development of the ULOs.
