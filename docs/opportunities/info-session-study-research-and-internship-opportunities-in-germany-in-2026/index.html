Info Session: Study, Research and Internship Opportunities in Germany in 2027-28

UW Bothell Events

Join DAAD – German Academic Exchange Service to learn details on their scholarship and grant programs in support of student study, research and internships in Germany in 2027-28 . The info session will be held virtually on September 30th and will start 12:30 pm ET / 9:30 am PT . DAAD has funding programs to support students at all stages (Bachelor, Master’s and PhD) in all fields of study . No prior knowledge of German is required.
