Build with Google - Life Sciences Deep Dive

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Build with Google – Life Sciences Deep Dive is a half-day, hands-on workshop for UW researchers exploring practical applications of AI in life sciences and scientific discovery. The event will begin with an overview of Google’s AI tools and platforms, including examples of how AI can support research workflows such as grant compliance and scientific discovery. Participants will then take part in a hands-on life sciences lab using pre-configured Google Cloud environments with NVIDIA GPU infrastructure.
