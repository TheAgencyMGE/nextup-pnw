From Idea to App Using Everyday Language: An Introduction to Vibe Coding (Corvallis Campus)

Oregon State University Events

AI changes who can build apps. You no longer need to spend months to years writing code; now you just need to spend minutes to hours communicating your ideas. This one-hour, hands-on workshop introduces "vibe coding", which is the use of conversational language to direct AI to build functional applications such as, say, software as a service (SaaS) with ecommerce integration. Discover how to turn your domain expertise into personal or global apps for desktops, tablets, mobile devices, webpages, or web extensions. Bring your laptop to the workshop.
