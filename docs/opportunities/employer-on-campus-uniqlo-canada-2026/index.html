Employer on Campus: UNIQLO Canada

UBC Career Centre

Interested in launching your career with a global retailer? Join UNIQLO Canada to learn about our UNIQLO Management Candidate (UMC) Program! During this session, we’ll introduce UNIQLO’s culture, leadership development opportunities, and career pathways for new graduates. Attendees will have the chance to learn more about the role, ask questions directly to our team, network with representatives, and submit their resumes for consideration. This is a great opportunity for students interested in leadership, business, operations, and building a long-term career with a global organization.
