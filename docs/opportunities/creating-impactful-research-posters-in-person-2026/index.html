Creating Impactful Research Posters (in-person)

Boise State University Events

Creating a research poster is something you are likely to do at some point of your research journey. Whether you are a pro, or whether you have never created a research poster, join this workshop to learn some tips and tricks for creating an effective research poster. This workshop will especially be useful for students presenting, or planning to present a poster at the 2026 SL/VIP Fall Expo. RSVP via Campus Groups: https://cglink.me/2yk/r398895
