Winter 2026 Grad Fair

Boise State University Events

Graduates are invited to the Grad Fair on October 20 and 21 from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Bronco Shop in the Student Union Building. Grad Fair is a great opportunity to celebrate and prep for your upcoming graduation ceremony. Swing by to meet campus resources, grab some food, film your grad shout-out video, and order your invitations and class ring. Students who visit every booth will be entered to win prize giveaways!
