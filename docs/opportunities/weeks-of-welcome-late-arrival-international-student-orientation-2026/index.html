Weeks of Welcome: Late Arrival International Student Orientation

UBC Okanagan Events

Missed Jump Start or arrived at UBCO after the main orientation? Join us for our Late Arrival International Student Orientation to get settled, connected, and feel more confident starting your journey at UBCO and in Canada! The session will also cover key information to help you settle in, including immigration and study permit requirements, working in Canada, health insurance, Canadian classroom culture, academic and language supports, housing, banking, transportation, life in Kelowna, community connections, and international student advising and wellness resources. Come with your questions, meet other students, and get the information you need for a successful start at UBCO!
