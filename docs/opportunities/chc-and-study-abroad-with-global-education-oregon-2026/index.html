CHC and Study Abroad with Global Education Oregon

University of Oregon Events

First-year Clark Honors College students are invited to learn from the Global Education Oregon (GEO) team about how to be prepared to study abroad during your time at UO. Your CHC first-year advisors will also be there to talk about working it into your graduation plan and how course equivalencies will work.
