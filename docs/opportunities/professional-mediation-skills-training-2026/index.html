Professional Mediation Skills Training

University of Washington Campus Calendar

The University of Washington School of Law 36-Hour Professional Mediation Skills Training is an immersive, skills-based program designed for professionals who want to strengthen their conflict management and mediation expertise within a rigorous academic and practical framework. Taught by UW School of Law faculty and experienced mediation practitioners, the training integrates evidence-based theory with extensive hands-on learning. Over 36 hours, participants develop the ability to assess conflict dynamics, build rapport with parties, manage strong emotions, facilitate structured dialogue, and guide negotiations toward durable, self-determined agreements.
