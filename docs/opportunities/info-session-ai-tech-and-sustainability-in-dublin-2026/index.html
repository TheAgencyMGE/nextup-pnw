Info Session: AI, Tech, and Sustainability in Dublin

University of Oregon Events

Join the info session for AI, Tech, and Sustainability in Dublin . We'll talk about the program in more detail — and get to meet the UO faculty member Michael Jordan from the Lundquist College of Business. You will also learn about the excursions: overnight in Galway, visits to data centers, many guest speakers and experts in the field. This event is part of International Education Month (IEM) programming. View the full calendar of IEM events here: https://international.uoregon.edu/IEM .
