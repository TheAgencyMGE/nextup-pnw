Effective Program Assessment: The "Key" to Key Findings (zoom)

Boise State University Events

You've identified - and possibly already collected - measures of student learning in your program. But how do you use those measures to determine how well students are achieving your program learning outcomes (and, as a result, the ""Key Findings"" for your PAR report)? In this workshop, you will have the opportunity to connect with other educators about how they analyze student performance on their program assessment measures and use that information to develop conclusions about student learning.
