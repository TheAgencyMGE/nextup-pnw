Design and Performing Arts Club Fair

Oregon State University Events

Looking for a community to foster your artistic talents? The Design and Performing Arts Club Fair will help you find just that! Connect with the various Design and Performing Arts clubs at OSU. Learn about the various competitions, events, and performances that you could be a part of. Find a close-knit community where you can express your talents or learn new skills. We'll be at the MU Quad between 11 a.m. - 4 p.m. Stop by and check out the clubs when you're free! Hosted by OSU Gulab Jammin', the premier South Asian Fusion A Cappella Team at Oregon State.
