Dissertation Defense: Stanley Akor

Boise State University Events

Stanley Akor Dissertation Defense Title: Advancing Winter Hydrometeorological Prediction: From Physics-Based Modeling to Artificial Intelligence Abstract: Winter hydrometeorological prediction remains challenging because of complex interactions among atmospheric forcing, precipitation phase, atmosphere-land interaction, model uncertainty, sparse observations, and computational cost. This dissertation investigates these challenges by progressing from physics-based numerical modeling to artificial intelligence approaches.
