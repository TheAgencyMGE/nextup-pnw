UBC Midwifery Info Sessions

Faculty of Medicine – Department of Family Practice

Interested in becoming a midwife? Join UBC Midwifery for one of our Fall 2026 Information Sessions to learn more about the Bachelor of Midwifery program, admissions and the student experience at UBC. Prospective students are invited to attend one of three sessions. Two sessions will be offered online via Zoom, and a third will be held in person at BC Women’s Hospital in Vancouver. There will also be an opportunity to ask questions and connect with the UBC Midwifery team. Fall 2026 Information Sessions October 16, 6–7 p.m. PDT — Online via Zoom October 23, 6–7 p.m. PDT — Online via Zoom October 30, 6–7 p.m. PDT — In person at BC Women’s Hospital, SHY E404, Vancouver Registration is required.
