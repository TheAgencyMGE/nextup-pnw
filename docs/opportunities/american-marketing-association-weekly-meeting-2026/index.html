We host workshops, networking opportunities, guest speakers, career events, and social activities designed to help students grow their skills; like expanding their portfolios, and connecting with industry professionals. All UO students are welcome—no marketing experience required! Come connect, learn, and grow with us. We hope to see you at our next event!
