OSU Summer Session: Querétaro, Mexico Linguistic and Cultural Immersion Program (INFO SESSION)

Oregon State University Events

Join OSU study abroad, program coordinator Jennifer Jewett for an information session to learn more about this six-week, 12-credit, summer study abroad program in Querétaro, México. These Querétaro linguistic and cultural immersion programs are designed for students entering 200*, 300 or 400-level Spanish language classes. Upper-division history and literature classes are also offered. Classes are small and taught in Spanish by local Mexican professors and instructors. The Centro Cultural of the UNAM hosts the academic program. The Querétaro program, celebrating its 41st year in 2027, is among the most affordable of programs offered by OSU in a Spanish-speaking country.
