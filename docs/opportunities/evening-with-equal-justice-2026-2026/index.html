Evening with Equal Justice 2026

Seattle University Events

Join Seattle University School of Law’s Access to Justice Institute and Public Interest Law Foundation for the 2026 Evening with Equal Justice, our largest on campus public interest networking event of the year! The evening will feature a keynote address from Riddhi Mukhopadhyay ’09, Executive Director of the Sexual Violence Law Center, followed by interactive roundtable networking between law students and members of the legal community. During three 15-minute networking rounds, attorneys will rotate among small groups of students organized around practice areas and interests.
