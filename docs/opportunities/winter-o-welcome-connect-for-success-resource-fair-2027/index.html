Winter O Welcome: Connect for Success Resource Fair

UBC Okanagan Events

Ready to kick off the new term and make progress on your New Year’s goals? Join us at our resource fair to connect or reconnect with the wide range of services, programs, and supports available at UBCO! Whether you’re looking to boost your academic success, prioritize your wellbeing or build your sticker collection, this is your chance to discover the resources that can help you thrive this semester. What to expect: This is a drop-in event running from noon to 2:30 pm. Participants can expect a higher level of noise, and the space may be busy and somewhat crowded at times. Who can attend: New-to-UBC undergraduate students, graduate students, visiting students—all students in their first year
