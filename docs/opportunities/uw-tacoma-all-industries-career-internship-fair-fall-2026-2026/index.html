UW Tacoma All Industries Career & Internship Fair - Fall 2026

Our bi-annual Career & Internship Fair is an opportunity for all students to network with employers representing various industries. This fair is an opportunity to make connections with employers and students, to develop your personal brand, and to learn about available job and internship opportunities. This year's fair will be held in person in William W. Philip Hall (WPH) on UW Tacoma's campus.
