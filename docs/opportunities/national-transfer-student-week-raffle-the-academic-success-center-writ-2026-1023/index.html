National Transfer Student Week Raffle @ the Academic Success Center & Writing Center

Oregon State University Events

Happy National Transfer Student Week! If you're a transfer student, visit the Academic Success Center and/or the Writing Center to be entered into a raffle for one of two productivity baskets. Each basket includes a planner, erasable pens, highlighters, planner stickers, ASC stickers, a lens cloth cleaner, a fidget or pomodoro timer, and more!! Scan the QR code in the Writing Center and/or the Academic Success Center. Visit both centers to be entered twice! And then check out our resources - we love OSU transfer students!
