Thesis Defense: Owen Walsh

Boise State University Events

Please join us for Owen's thesis defense on Wednesday, October 7th at 3 p.m. in MCMR 106 . Owen Walsh Thesis Defense Title: Variability of Howitzer Artillery Infrasound Recorded in Little Cottonwood Canyon, Utah Abstract: For 20 years, avalanche forecasters in Little Cottonwood Canyon, Utah, have used infrasound to detect avalanches and monitor explosive avalanche control activity. One control technique, howitzer artillery, can fail to detonate on impact, which creates a hazard. Snow often buries infrasound sensors, which alters the recorded amplitude and spectral content of howitzer explosions by muffling incoming sound, which can make it difficult to confirm that ordnance exploded.
