Fundamentals For Startups: AI investment trends in venture capital

University of Washington Campus Calendar

This year's Fundamentals for Startups series is proudly presented by Curinos. Please register for this event on our Luma calendar . Elevators and doors open at 11:30 AM and will close promptly at 12:10 PM. Lecture: Jon Prentice from Silicon Valley Bank will share key takeaways from SVB's State of the Markets report, with a focus on how AI is reshaping venture capital investment. He'll unpack the growing phenomenon of "AI washing"—where companies market themselves as AI-driven without substantive AI technology—and discuss how investors are becoming sharper at distinguishing genuine AI innovators from the hype, rewarding them accordingly.
