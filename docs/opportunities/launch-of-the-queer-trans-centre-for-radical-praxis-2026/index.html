Launch of the Queer Trans Centre for Radical Praxis

Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice

Join us for the launch of the Queer Trans Centre for Radical Praxis (QTC) at the University of British Columbia. The launch brings together leading scholars and artists in queer and trans studies for a panel conversation on the current state and future directions of queer and trans scholarship, art, and praxis.
