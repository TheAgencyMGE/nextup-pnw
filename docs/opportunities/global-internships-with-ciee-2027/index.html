Global Internships with CIEE

UW Bothell Events

Gain professional experience and build career skills through a CIEE global internship! CIEE, a UW-approved study abroad partner, offers for-credit (unpaid) internship placements across a variety of disciplines in 37 world locations. Madison Breschini, CIEE advisor for UW students, will go over available options, the application process, and scholarships. Start your journey at: https://www.ciee.org/go-abroad/college-study-abroad/internships . Read about the experience of UW Bothell alumna, Sumaya Yusuf, who interned with an e-sports company in Seoul , S. Korea.
