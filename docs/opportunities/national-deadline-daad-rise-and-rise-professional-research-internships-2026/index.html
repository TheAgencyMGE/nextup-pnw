National Deadline: DAAD RISE and RISE Professional Research Internships in Germany

UW Tacoma Events

By November 30 , interested students can apply for internships at universities, companies, and research institutions in Germany. RISE is a scholarship program by DAAD - the German Academic Exchange Service - and offers research internships in Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Earth Science, Engineering and Physics or a closely related field. Students can enhance their knowledge through hands-on research, gain international experience, and discover Germany’s unique research landscape. Working language is English, generally.
