Theodore Jacobsen Observatory Open House

Welcome Welcome to the web page of the public events program at the Theodor Jacobsen Observatory (TJO) of the University of Washington. Come and join us for open houses where we offer a view through our 1895 telescope on clear dark nights. In addition, enjoy evening talks and other activities during your visit for people of all ages, from 6 to 100. Tickets are free. Programs normally take place at the observatory on the first and third Tuesday evenings of April through September, rain or shine.
