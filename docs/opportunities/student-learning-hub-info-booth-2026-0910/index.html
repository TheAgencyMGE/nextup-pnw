Student Learning Hub Info Booth

UBC Okanagan Events

Are you interested in learning about the free learning supports for all UBCO students provided by the Student Learning Hub? Stop by the Student Learning Hub table outside the west entrance to the Library building (around the corner from Tim Hortons before the Commons) to say “Hi!” and ask all your questions about how we can support you in achieving your own personal learning goals through the wide range of learning supports we offer! We will have stickers, sweet treats and lots of information about our free writing and tutoring supports in a huge range of subjects. Plus, you can learn about how our professional learning strategist can help you reach your highest potential.
