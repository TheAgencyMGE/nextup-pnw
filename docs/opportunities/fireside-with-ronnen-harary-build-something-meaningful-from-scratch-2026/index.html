Fireside with Ronnen Harary: Build Something Meaningful from Scratch

University of Oregon Events

What does it take to turn an idea into a successful business? Join Ronnen Harary, co-founder of Spin Master and co-creator of PAW Patrol and Bakugan, for a fireside conversation about entrepreneurship, building a business, and bringing ideas to life. This moderated discussion will be followed by a Q&A. Harary will share insights from his entrepreneurial journey as part of his No Experience Necessary campus tour, which encourages aspiring entrepreneurs to take the first step toward building something of their own. This event is co-hosted by UO Women in Business, Oregon Founders Club, and UO American Marketing Association.
