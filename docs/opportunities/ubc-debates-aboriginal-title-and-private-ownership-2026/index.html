UBC Debates: Aboriginal Title and Private Ownership

UBC Debates

Aboriginal Title and Private Ownership January 26, 2026, 7–8:30 pm UBC Robson Square Theatre | 800 Robson St, Vancouver, BC ( map ) Get notified when tickets are available Recent court decisions on Aboriginal land title and their impact on private property has brought to the forefront the longstanding question of how to resolve competing land claims in British Columbia. While courts may recognize both interests, significant questions remain about what coexistence looks like in practice. Join us on November 16 as UBC Debates brings together four prominent voices to debate whether Aboriginal Title and Private Ownership Can Coexist.
