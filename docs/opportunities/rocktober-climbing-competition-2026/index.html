Rocktober Climbing Competition

Boise State University Events

Come join us for our 31st annual Boise State Climbing Competition at the Rec Center. This years Rocktober Climbing Competition is a chance to hangout with cool people, challenge yourself, and win some prizes. No matter your experience level, come climb with us! By signing up you'll also be receiving a climbing competition t-shirt. Event is from 12pm- 5pm. Registration Cost: $25
