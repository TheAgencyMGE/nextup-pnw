Creative Writing Reading Series: Michael Copperman

University of Oregon Events

Join the Creative Writing program for a fiction reading by Michael Copperman. Copperman’s work has appeared in The Oxford-American , Guernica , The Sun , Creative Nonfiction , Boston Review , Salon , Gulf Coast , Triquarterly , Kenyon Review and Copper Nickel , among others. His memoir Teacher: Two Years in the Mississippi Delta (University Press of Mississippi 2017) was a finalist for the 2018 Oregon Book Award in CNF. His new book, Seeking Kenny, is about the extremes of the American subculture of wrestling as seen through the story of five-time national champion Oregon wrestler Kenny Cox’s pilgrimage deep into the wilderness of Kauai’s Na Pali Coast (University of Iowa Press 2026).
