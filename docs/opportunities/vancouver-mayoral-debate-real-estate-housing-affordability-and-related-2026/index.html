Vancouver Mayoral Debate: Real Estate, Housing Affordability, and Related Issues

UBC Sauder School of Business

UBC Sauder’s Centre for Urban Economics is hosting its third Vancouver Mayoral Debate in coordination with the Allard School of Law and UBC’s Housing Research Collaborative. As always, our focus will be on real estate-related topics, including zoning, housing affordability, commercial uses, homelessness, and public safety. This will be an opportunity for the major candidates for Mayor to explain their plans and perspectives in depth. As always, there will be time for networking and refreshments before and after the debate.
