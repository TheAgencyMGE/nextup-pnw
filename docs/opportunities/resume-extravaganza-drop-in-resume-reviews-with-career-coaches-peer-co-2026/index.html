Resume Extravaganza! (Drop-In Resume Reviews with Career Coaches & Peer Coaches)

University of Oregon Events

Did you know you can have someone review your resume before the Fall Career & Internship Expo on 10/22 ? Drop-in with a career readiness coach or peer coach in Tykeson Hall Commons to get feedback on your resume! Free cookies & hot chocolate too :) Don’t have a resume? Come learn how to make one! This University Career Center event is part of the 2026 Fall Career Readiness Week sponsored by Enterprise Mobility. To learn more about all of the week's events visit http://career.uoregon.edu/events
