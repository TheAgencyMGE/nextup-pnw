Beyond Prompting: Creating Teaching Materials with Claude Skills and NotebookLM

Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology

This 90-minute online hands-on workshop explores how generative AI can support the development of teaching materials and curriculum resources in higher education. Moving beyond prompting and generating outputs, participants will experiment with richer AI-supported workflows that combine source materials, iterative refinement, research tools, lightweight agents, and structured inputs such as syllabi and learning outcomes to generate activities, assessments, lesson plans, and curriculum maps.
