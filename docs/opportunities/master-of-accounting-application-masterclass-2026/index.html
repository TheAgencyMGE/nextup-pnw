Master of Accounting Application Masterclass

University of Oregon Events

Join admission representatives to learn more about the 10-month Master of Accounting (MAcc) program. This session offers a unique opportunity to learn about the MAcc program, admission requirements, and the application process. You'll also have an opportunity to ask the team questions before the MAcc early decision deadline on October 15, 2026. This is the final deadline to start the MAcc in winter or spring 2027, and the early decision deadline to start the MAcc in fall 2027.
