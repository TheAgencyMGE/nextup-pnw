SOMD Ensemble Auditions - Cello and Classical Bass

University of Oregon Events

Ensemble auditions for the 2026-27 academic year are open to students from all majors. Personnel for the various orchestras, bands, and wind ensembles will be formed from these auditions. Participation in the Symphony Orchestra, Wind Ensemble, and Symphonic Band requires in-person auditions. Participation in Campus Band and Campus Orchestra does not require an audition. Please visit the ensemble website for audition information, including excerpts (available August 1st) and sign-ups. Dr. Jacobs is your contact for questions, djacobs@uoregon.edu
