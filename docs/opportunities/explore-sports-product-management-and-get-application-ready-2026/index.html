Explore Sports Product Management and Get Application-Ready

University of Oregon Events

Ready to turn your passion for sport and outdoor products into a career? Join the University of Oregon Sports Product Management (SPM) program for our first virtual information session of the new recruiting cycle. Discover what makes the SPM master's program unique and how our immersive, industry-connected approach prepares students to break into and build careers in the sport and outdoor product industry. During this session, we'll cover: What sets the UO Sports Product Management program apart How the program prepares students for careers in the sport and outdoor product industry An overview of the new application Requirements for applying Key application deadlines and important next steps
