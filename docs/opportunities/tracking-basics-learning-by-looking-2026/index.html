Tracking Basics: Learning by Looking

UW Bothell Events

Tracking Basics: Learning by Looking Co-presented by CoSEE & Finn Hill Neighborhood Alliance Have you ever pondered a print in the mud? Have you heard howls in response to the fire trucks? Have you noticed some "trimming" in your garden you don't remember doing? You're noticing the signs of the many species of wildlife that live alongside us! Take a dive into the world of tracking with "Tracking Basics: Learning by Looking!" Uncover who lives here by investigating the signs they leave long after they are gone.
