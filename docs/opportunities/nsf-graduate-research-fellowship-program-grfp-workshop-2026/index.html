NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP) Workshop

Boise State University Events

The NSF GRFP Workshop is a prestigious fellowship program for students who demonstrate the potential to make impactful research contributions in STEM or STEM education. The five-year fellowship provides three years of financial support with an annual stipend of $37,000.
