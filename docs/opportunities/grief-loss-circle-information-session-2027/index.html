Grief & Loss Circle — Information Session

UBC Okanagan Events

Grief is not limited to mourning the death of someone. It can also arise from many experiences of loss, including changes in identity, relationships, hopes and dreams, belonging, or a sense of stability. In any form, grief can feel isolating. We don’t always have spaces where we can talk about what we have lost, feel understood, or connect with others navigating similar experiences. Join a small, supportive circle facilitated by UBCO counsellor Patricia Gómez to explore grief, loss and change in community.
