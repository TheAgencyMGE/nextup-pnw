Orientation for Postdoctoral Fellows

Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies

Are you a new postdoctoral fellow on campus? The Postdoctoral Fellow Office’s tri-annual Orientation for Postdoctoral Fellows is designed to welcome new postdocs to UBC, introduce them to key aspects of the University, and provide opportunities to connect with fellow postdocs and various campus units. This informative session will cover essential academic and campus-related topics to help postdocs and their families make the most of your postdoctoral experience at UBC. Presentations will be given by representatives from Health, Wellbeing and Benefits, Payroll, the Office of the Vice-President, Research & Innovation, the Postdoctoral Fellows Office (PDFO), and the UBC Postdoctoral Association
