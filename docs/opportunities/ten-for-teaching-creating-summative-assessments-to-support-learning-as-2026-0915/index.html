Ten for Teaching: Creating summative assessments to support learning (ASYNCHRONOUS)

Boise State University Events

Summative assessments, e.g., end-of-unit or end-of-semester papers or projects, are not only useful to us as instructors as a means of evaluating student work but can serve as opportunities for learning. This workshop will support you in developing the kinds of summative assessments which align with your course learning outcomes and provide learning opportunities. In order to support both learning and motivation, we’ll also explore how to create authentic assessments which offer students choices and are presented transparently.
