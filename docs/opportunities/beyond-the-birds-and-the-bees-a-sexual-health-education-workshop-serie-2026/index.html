Beyond the Birds and the Bees: A Sexual Health Education Workshop Series for UBC Graduate students

UBC Sexual Violence Prevention and Response Office (SVPRO)

Join us for a 6-week, free, interactive Lunch & Learn workshop series about sex positivity, pleasure, gender and sexuality, safer sex practices, and more! Workshop starts on October 7th and runs every Wednesday (excluding November 11th) until November 18th, 2026 from 12:30-2:00pm. Students are welcome to come anytime from 12:00pm to enjoy mingling, a catered lunch, and goodies! Sign up indicates that students can attend all sessions. Location is: SVPRO Office, 6180 Walter Gage Road . Room 2110, Brock Commons South. Sessions are for registered UBC graduate students only, and require sign-up via a Qualtrics link: https://www.grad.ubc.ca/current-students/professional-development/about-gps
