Time, Work, & Family: Building a Realistic Success Plan for Online Learning

Oregon State University Events

Feeling stretched thin between work, family responsibilities, and school? You're not alone. In this interactive workshop, we'll explore practical strategies for balancing the many roles and responsibilities that come with being an online student. Through guided activities, you'll assess how your time is currently being spent, identify realistic opportunities for studying, consider your energy levels and workload, and create a personalized success plan that fits your life. You'll leave with a clearer understanding of your weekly commitments, strategies for overcoming common obstacles, and practical tools to help you stay organized, focused, and confident throughout the term.
