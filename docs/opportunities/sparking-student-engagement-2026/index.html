Sparking Student Engagement

UBC Okanagan Events

If you are looking for simple techniques to increase student engagement for in-person and synchronous online classes, then don’t miss this hands-on workshop. Participants will experience the impact of incorporating into class time moments for connection, movement, creativity, curiosity and fun. You will engage in playful learning and innovative thinking to apply engagement techniques where they are most needed—where students feel stuck and unmotivated. Participants will come away with ideas for tweaking existing activities to foster affirming, inquiry-based, and joyful classes. Register here
