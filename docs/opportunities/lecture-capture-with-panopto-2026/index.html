Lecture Capture with Panopto

Panopto is the UW lecture capture tool that allows you to create and share your classroom lectures. In this workshop you will learn how to record lectures or presentations, screen captures, and discover Panopto's editing and sharing capabilities. Learning Outcomes Participants will be able to record and upload video content using Panopto's desktop application and web interface. Participants will be able to perform basic video editing within Panopto and share their recordings with students. Facilitated by Todd Conaway, Instructional Designer
