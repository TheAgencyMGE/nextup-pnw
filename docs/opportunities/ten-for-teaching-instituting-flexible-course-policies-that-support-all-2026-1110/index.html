Ten for Teaching: Instituting flexible course policies that support all learners (ASYNCHRONOUS)

Boise State University Events

During the COVID-19 pandemic many educators loosened their course policies (e.g., around attendance and late work). Several years later, some of that flexibility has remained, with educators noting both positive and negative impacts on student learning. This dichotomy leads many of us to wonder what the right balance of flexibility is. In this workshop, we will begin by exploring the demographics of Boise State students and how those might impact the choices we make. In addition, you will have the opportunity to discuss your thoughts and assumptions about course policies, review different policy models, and consider what policies best support your students' learning.
