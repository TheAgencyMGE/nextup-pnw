Sustainability Ecosystem Mapping Gathering

UW Bothell Events

Are you a faculty or staff member engaged in sustainability-related teaching, research, operations, community partnerships, or student programs ? Join colleagues from across UW Bothell for an interactive gathering to explore how our work connects to the broader regional sustainability ecosystem. Together, participants will share knowledge, build new connections, and help identify the relationships, partnerships, networks, opportunities, and gaps that shape UW Bothell's contribution to environmental, economic, and social sustainability. Insights from this work will inform the development of a Sustainability Ecosystem Map and support UW Bothell's Carnegie Sustainability Classification efforts.
