Island Resilience: Exploring Health, Sustainability, & Food Security in Puerto Rico (INFO SESSION)

Oregon State University Events

Join faculty leaders Rachel Jones and Wanda Crannell to learn more about this 1-week, study abroad opportunity in Puertio Rico, spring break 2027. This course offers a high-impact, three-credit faculty-led experience in Puerto Rico. Students will explore the island’s unique environmental, economic, and cultural landscape while working on community-based projects with local schools, government agencies, NGOs, and the University of Puerto Rico–Mayagüez. The program focuses on food security, climate change adaptation, coastal resilience, biodiversity conservation, community health, and sustainable development.
