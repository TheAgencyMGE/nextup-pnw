2026 International Invasive Species and Climate Change Conference (IISCCC) - Day 1

University of Washington Campus Calendar

In collaboration with our partners in other regions, we invite you to register for the 2026 International Invasive Species and Climate Change Conference (IISCCC) ! This year’s theme is, “Redefining invasive species management in a changing world.” This conference is VIRTUAL allowing for more international attendance while reducing our carbon footprint. This FREE conference will be held online, December 1 and 2, from 10am–2pm PST / 11am–3pm MST . Sessions will include the following topics at the intersection of climate change and invasive species: Marine ecosystems Food security Extreme events Lessons from management Disease/One Health Please click on the link below to register for both days
