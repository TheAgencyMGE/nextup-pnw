Women Trailblazers Across Disciplines - Women's Leadership Panel With Wismer Professor

Seattle University Events

Hear from inspiring women leaders in the Pacific Northwest on how they succeeded in primarily male dominated spaces. From healthcare to engineering, venture capital to sports communications, engage the shared wisdom of women who have ascended to positions of influence. This hour of positive reflection will offer insights into how key elements of success transcend career paths. Learn about both overarching mindsets and practical advice from extraordinary industry leaders. Panelists: - Agnieszka Miguel , Professor and Department Chair of Seattle University Electrical Engineering Department: Dr. Agnieszka Miguel is Professor and Chair of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seattle University
