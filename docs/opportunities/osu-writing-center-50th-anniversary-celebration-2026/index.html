OSU Writing Center 50th Anniversary Celebration

Oregon State University Events

The OSU Writing Center turns 50 this year! Founded as the Communication Skills Center in 1976, the OSU Writing Center will celebrate its 50th anniversary on Monday, Oct. 19 from 1 to 3 p.m. This event will kick off a week of celebration in the Writing Studio in the Valley Library, main floor. We’ll have cake, refreshments, and remarks from current and former Writing Center staff. Feel free to arrive at any time between 1- 3 pm. Brief remarks at 1:30 p.m.
