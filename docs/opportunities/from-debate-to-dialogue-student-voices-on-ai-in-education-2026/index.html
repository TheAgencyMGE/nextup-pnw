From debate to dialogue: Student voices on AI in education

Boise State University Events

Do the benefits of AI-assisted grading outweigh the costs? A student debate and dialogue with Boise State’s Talkin’ Broncos tackles this question. This live debate is a unique opportunity for the campus community. The Talkin' Broncos, Boise State's decorated speech and debate team, are partnering with the School for the Digital Future to center student perspectives and foster discussion around the complex topic of AI's place in education. Pizza will be provided to guests and a collaborative group discussion, facilitated by member of the Talkin' Broncos will follow the debate arguments. Debate prompt: The benefits of AI-assisted grading outweigh the costs. Speaker bios Affirmative - Jaxon Hol
