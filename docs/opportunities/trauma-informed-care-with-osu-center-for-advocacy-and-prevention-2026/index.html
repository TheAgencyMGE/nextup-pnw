Trauma-Informed Care with OSU Center for Advocacy and Prevention

Oregon State University Events

Trauma-informed care is for everyone. We have all experienced trauma and interact daily with people who have experienced trauma. This training offers practical tools for: Creating learning environments where participants can discuss difficult topics and tend to their own needs Supporting yourself and others when they are triggered Understanding how trauma affects our responses, interactions, and ability to engage with services and support Responding to trauma disclosures including interpersonal or gender-based violence. This event is hosted by the College of Earth, Ocean, and Atmospheric Sciences Graduate Student Association, in partnership with the College's Community of Practice.
