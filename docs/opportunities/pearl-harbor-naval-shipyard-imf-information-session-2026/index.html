Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard & IMF Information Session

Oregon State University Events

Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard & IMF is the largest industrial employer in the State of Hawai'i. With a workforce of over 7,000 civilians, military personnel, and contractors, we work to repair and maintain our nuclear-powered submarines and ships to keep our Pacific fleet “Fit to Fight.” We employ over 1,000 engineers to troubleshoot and analyze our engineering problems that arise on each project. Attend our information session to speak with our recruiters! All engineering majors welcome. https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Home/Shipyards/PHNS-IMF/
