Job Shadow Day Info Session

University of Oregon Events

Interested in exploring career possibilities? Join us to learn about Job Shadow Day . This unique opportunity connects students with local professionals for a day of workplace observation, career exploration, and networking. At this info session, you'll learn: What Job Shadow Day is and how it works How to apply and get matched with a site host What to expect during your shadow experience Tips for making the most of your day Whether you're exploring career options, curious about a particular industry, or looking to gain firsthand insight into the world of work, this session will help you take the next step with confidence.
