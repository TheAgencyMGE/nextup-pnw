Fall Career Day

Oregon State University Events

Join the Marine Education Team for Fall Career Day at Hatfield Marine Science Center! Designed for high school students, this event offers a chance to connect with STEM professionals, dive into hands-on activities, and go behind the scenes to explore research labs and other spaces at Hatfield. Registration is $30 per student. Please note that program fees are fully refundable if cancelation notification is sent to the Marine Education Coordinator by Oct. 15. Registration fees are non-refundable if cancellation after Oct. 15 with limited exceptions on case by case basis. Parents and teachers are welcome to learn more and register their students here: https://seagrant.oregonstate.edu/visitor-c
