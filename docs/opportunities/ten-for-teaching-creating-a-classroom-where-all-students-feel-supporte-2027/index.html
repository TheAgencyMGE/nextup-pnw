Ten for Teaching: Creating a classroom where all students feel supported in learning (ASYNCHRONOUS)

Boise State University Events

To support student learning, we, as instructors, should welcome students, get to know them as people, validate their abilities, demystify our expectations of them, and help them learn deeply. This workshop will explore how we can create this kind of learning environment. The first step in this process is building and maintaining trusting relationships with students. From there, we will explore how to cultivate a learning environment where every student feels they belong, matter, and can thrive. Finally, we will consider how to intentionally structure class sessions in support of learning.
