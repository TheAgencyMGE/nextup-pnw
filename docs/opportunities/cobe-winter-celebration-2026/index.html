COBE Winter Celebration

Boise State University Events

Join us for the COBE Winter Celebration on Thursday, December 3, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM in the Jackson Commons! Whether you’re wrapping up your final term or walking across the stage this winter, it’s time to celebrate all your hard work this fall. Stop by to grab free cookies and coffee, snap a photo at our photo station, and mingle with fellow COBE students, faculty, and staff. Want to win some extra swag? Share a brief student success story about a recent career win with us at the event (like landing an internship, securing a job offer, or acing an interview), and you'll be automatically entered into our raffle!
