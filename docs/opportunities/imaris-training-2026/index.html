Imaris Training

University of Oregon Events

Imaris for Core Training Turn your microscopy images into meaningful data. Join the BioFoundry for hands-on training with an Imaris specialist and explore powerful tools for 3D/4D visualization, segmentation, quantification, tracking, colocalization, and more. Morning: 9:00–11:00 AM | Afternoon: 1:00–3:00 PM The same training will be offered in both sessions. Bring your own data. The optional final 30 minutes will feature in-depth discussion and analysis of up to two user datasets per session. To participate, email biofoundry@uoregon.edu by September 22 with your dataset and analysis goal. Interested? Register today through the Imaris Software Training form .
