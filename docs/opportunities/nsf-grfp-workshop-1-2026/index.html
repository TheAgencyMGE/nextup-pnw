NSF-GRFP Workshop 1

Oregon State University Events

This is an introductory workshop providing attendees with an overview of the NSF GRFP. We'll describe the application process as well as important changes to the eligibility requirements and focus of the program, and cover strategies for crafting compelling proposals that target the specific expectations of the rubric. For accommodations related to a disability, please contact elise.hohensee@oregonstate.edu no later than September 11.
