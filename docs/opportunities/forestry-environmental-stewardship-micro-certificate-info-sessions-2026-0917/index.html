Forestry & Environmental Stewardship Micro-certificate Info Sessions

Faculty of Forestry & Environmental Stewardship

Designed for working professionals, UBC Forestry & Environmental Stewardship’s wide array of Micro-certificate programs offer an accessible opportunity to elevate your skills, engage with industry leaders and advance in your career. Delivered exclusively online, our micro-certificates are paving the way to providing quality, industry-aligned and accessible forestry education across Canada. Join one of the information sessions to hear from the program director to learn more.
