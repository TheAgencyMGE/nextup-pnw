The 20th Annual Earl W. Davie Symposium

Centre for Blood Research

The CBR is pleased to present its 20th Annual Earl W. Davie Symposium on November 12, 2026! The symposium is a full-day, hybrid event (held at UBC Robson Square and virtually ) that brings together international and local experts to discuss the latest advances in hemostasis, thrombosis, vascular biology, bleeding disorders, cardiovascular and neurologic diseases, and transfusion medicine. This year, our special guest speakers are John Weisel (UPenn), Jean Connors (Harvard), and Walter Kahr (UofT, SickKids). Registration is free . Food and drinks are provided for in-person attendees. Register for the event: https://ubc.ca1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_7WMD5xm0HCyLR4O PRESENT AT THE SYMPOSIUM – C
