Embodying Revolutionary Love: Somatic Healing and Collective Care Workshop

UBC Okanagan Events

Please note: this workshop is open to self-identified queer and trans students only. What is “revolutionary love,” and how do we practice loving ourselves and others in the midst of terrifying times? Trans, non-binary, and gender diverse individuals are invited to join trauma-healing expert and somatics teacher Kai Cheng Thom for a workshop in embodied practices for personal healing, collective care, and social change. In this session, Kai Cheng will offer participants an understanding of how somatics can be used as a resource for marginalized groups.
