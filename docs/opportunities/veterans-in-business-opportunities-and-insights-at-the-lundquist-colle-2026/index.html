Veterans in Business: Opportunities and Insights at the Lundquist College of Business

University of Oregon Events

In honor of Veterans Day, join the Lundquist College of Business Graduate Admissions Team for a special event highlighting the experiences of our veteran community. Hear from current students, University of Oregon VA representatives, and Lundquist alumni who have served in the military. This event is designed to provide valuable information and support to veterans admitted to, applying to, or interested in our programs. Connect with our veteran network and discover the unique opportunities available to you in our Lundquist Graduate Programs! This info session covers the following programs: The Oregon MBA Oregon Executive MBA Master of Accounting Master of Science in Finance Master of Science
