How to Turn Study Abroad Into a Career Advantage

University of Oregon Events

Studied abroad? Planning to? Wondering how the experience can actually help you in your career? Join us for the Study Abroad Career Panel. Hear directly from UO alumni working across different industries about how employers view study abroad and how students can talk about their international experiences in ways that are meaningful to future employers. The conversation will center on one important question: If you were hiring someone, how would you want that candidate to explain the value of studying abroad during an interview?
