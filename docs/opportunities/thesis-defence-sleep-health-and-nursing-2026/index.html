Thesis Defence: Sleep Health and Nursing

UBC Okanagan Events

Niki Soroush-Asghari, supervised by Dr. Elizabeth Keys, will defend their thesis titled “Sleep Health and Nursing: Considerations for the Transition to Practice” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Science in Nursing. An abstract for Niki Soroush-Asghari’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Please email Elizabeth.Keys@ubc.ca to receive the Zoom link for this defence.
