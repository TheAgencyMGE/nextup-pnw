UBC Engineering: Virtual Program Fair

UBC Okanagan Events

Explore your future in engineering from wherever you are! Join the UBC Engineering Virtual Program Fair to learn more about our 14 undergraduate engineering programs. You’ll have the chance to meet with professors, staff, and current students from each program, ask your questions about learning opportunities and career paths, and discover the right fit for you. All 14 of our UBC Engineering programs from the UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver campuses will be presenting and hosting four 30-minute sessions over the course of the event. Sessions will begin at 6 pm, 6:30 pm, 7 pm and 7:30 pm PT. Register here
