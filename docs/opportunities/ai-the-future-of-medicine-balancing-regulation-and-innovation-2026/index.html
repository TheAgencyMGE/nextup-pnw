AI & the Future of Medicine: Balancing Regulation and Innovation

Faculty of Medicine – Department of Medicine

We’re excited to invite you to AI & the Future of Medicine: Balancing Regulation and Innovation , a 2-day event hosted by the UBC DASH Research Cluster and MIT Critical Data . This cross-disciplinary symposium brings together clinicians, researchers, students, Indigenous leaders, policymakers, patients, industry partners, and members of the public to explore how artificial intelligence can improve health while being implemented safely, ethically, and responsibly.
