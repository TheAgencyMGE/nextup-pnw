Inclusive Teaching in Practice: UDL & Neurodiversity

Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology

Every classroom includes learners who think, process, communicate, and engage in different ways. How can we design learning experiences that better recognize and support this diversity? Join us for a three-hour, in-person workshop where we’ll explore neurodiversity and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) through conversation, reflection, and practical application. Bring your questions, experiences, and real teaching challenges; we’ll create space to learn from one another, explore strategies, and consider what inclusive teaching can look like in your own context.
