Pizza & Proposals with the Indigenous Strategic Initiatives Fund

Office of Indigenous Strategic Initiatives

The Indigenous Strategic Initiatives (“ISI”) Fund has announced its fifth funding cycle with a Call for Applications running from October 13, 2026, to January 14, 2027. The ISI Fund supports innovative and transformative projects led by UBC staff, faculty, and students across both campuses, that advance UBC’s Indigenous Strategic Plan and UBCO’s Declaration to Truth and Reconciliation Commitments.
