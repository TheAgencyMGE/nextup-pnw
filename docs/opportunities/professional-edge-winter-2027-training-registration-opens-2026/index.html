Professional Edge: Winter 2027 Training Registration Opens

University of Oregon Events

Want a skill you can name in an interview, plus proof that you have it? Learn an in-demand skill, earn a microcredential, and stand out to employers with Professional Edge! Professional Edge is the Lundquist College of Business's professional development initiative, shaped by what employers are hiring for right now. Each training digs into one specific skill through hands-on practice and wraps up with an assessment where you show what you can do. Complete a training and earn a microcredential, a verified digital badge you can add to LinkedIn and your resume, so you have something concrete to point to when recruiters ask what sets you apart.
