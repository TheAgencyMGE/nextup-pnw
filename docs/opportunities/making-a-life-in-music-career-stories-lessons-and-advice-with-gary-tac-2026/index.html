Making a Life in Music: Career stories, lessons, and advice with Gary Tackett and special guests

Boise State University Events

Date: Tuesday, October 27, 2026 Time: 12-1:30 PM While in Boise for their 5th Annual Smoke on the Water show at The Riverside Hotel’s iconic Sapphire Room, Gary Tackett and his all-star lineup of Tony Lindsay, Walfredo Reyes Jr., Myron Dove, and Eamonn Flynn have kindly agreed to join us for a special informal talk and Q&A session.
