Study and Stay Level 3 — Express Entry Points: Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)

UBC Okanagan Events

Planning to stay in Canada after graduation? Our Study and Stay Permanent Residence workshops are designed to help you navigate the immigration pathways, policies, and processes you’ll encounter when applying for Permanent Residence. This Level 3 Study & Stay session is a deep dive into the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) , the points-based system used by Canada’s federal Express Entry selection system to assess and rank profiles in the Express Entry pool. In this session, you’ll learn about the primary source for CRS guidance—the Ministerial Instructions—to identify strategic opportunities to increase your CRS ranking.
