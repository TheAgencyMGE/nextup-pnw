Yoga Workshop: Find Your Center

Oregon State University Events

Find Your Center | Balancing Postures with Layne Flath This Yoga Workshop is a practice session designed for all ranges of abilities, from beginners to more advanced practitioners. The class includes dynamic, flowing, and intelligently designed postural sequences to increase flexibility, strength, balance, and move internal energies. In each workshop, practitioners will learn and progress toward a different set of postures like inversions, backbends, and balancing postures. In this workshop, we'll practice balancing postures.
