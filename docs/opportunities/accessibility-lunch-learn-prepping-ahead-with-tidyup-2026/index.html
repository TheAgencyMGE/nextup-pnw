Accessibility Lunch & Learn: Prepping Ahead with TidyUP!

UW Tacoma Events

Many instructors know that TidyUP is a good tool for identifying and removing outdated files and other content from their courses, but did you know you can also use it to proactive plan ahead of Winter quarter or Spring quarter? We are pleased to welcome Aiden Sizemore and Kristen Guerra from the College of Education to talk about leveraging TidyUP in course prep. You will also learn how TidyUp helps improve the accessibility remediation scope in Ally and UDOIT, both of which are the most common tools instructors rely on to get their courses ready.
