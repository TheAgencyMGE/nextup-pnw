Communication and Media Studies Master's Information Session

University of Oregon Events

Explore how media and communication shape our world with a master's degree in Communication and Media Studies . In this research-focused graduate program, you'll work with internationally recognized faculty experts to develop advanced research and analytical skills, explore interdisciplinary perspectives, and examine the impact of media and communication on society. Whether you aspire to pursue doctoral studies or build a career in media research, government, law, or nonprofit organizations, you’ll gain the knowledge and expertise to make an impact through an ever-evolving media landscape.
