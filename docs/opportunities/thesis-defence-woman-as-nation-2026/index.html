Thesis Defence: Woman as Nation

UBC Okanagan Events

Roopkatha Misra, supervised by Dr. Jennifer Gustar, will defend their thesis titled “Woman as Nation: Gendered Nationalism in Three Indian Novels” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Arts in English. An abstract for Roopkatha Misra’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Please email jennifer.gustar@ubc.ca to receive the Zoom link for this defence.
