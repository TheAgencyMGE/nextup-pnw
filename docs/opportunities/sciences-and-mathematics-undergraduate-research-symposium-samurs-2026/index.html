Sciences and Mathematics Undergraduate Research Symposium (SAMURS)

UW Tacoma Events

Come join Sciences and Mathematics Faculty, Staff and Students at this year’s Autumn 2026 Sciences and Mathematics Undergraduate Research Symposium (SAMURS)! The University of Washington Tacoma Environmental Sciences, Biomedical Sciences, Environmental Sustainability, and Mathematics majors host an annual Environmental Research Symposium, Sciences and Mathematics Undergraduate Research Symposium (SAMURS). This event showcases the capstone research projects of our graduating students. This year we have a number of poster presentations by our undergraduate students, covering a broad range of questions and projects related to the environment.
