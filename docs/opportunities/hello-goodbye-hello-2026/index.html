Hello, Goodbye, Hello

Opening Reception: Friday, September 25 On View: June 1 - December 18, 2026 Hello, Goodbye, Hello is an exhibition of work in the Cornish Alumni Gallery by recent graduates from the Art BFA class of 2026. This show features a wide range of mediums and approaches including painting, photography, sculpture, printmaking, and installation. Each of the 10 artists have a distinct creative voice and relationship to their materials and subjects, that together offer a complex collection of contemporary perspectives and concerns.
