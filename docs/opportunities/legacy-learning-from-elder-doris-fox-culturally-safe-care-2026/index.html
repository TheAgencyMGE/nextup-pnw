Legacy Learning from Elder Doris Fox: Culturally Safe Care

Department of Emergency Medicine – Digital Emergency Medicine – iCON program

UBC Digital Emergency Medicine and VCH Indigenous Health are holding an upcoming session titled “Legacy Learning from Elder Doris Fox: Culturally Safe Care” on T hursday, November 19 at 12 p.m . Please note that this event takes a different approach from other Indigenous Health Rounds. Elder Doris Fox passed away on May 21, 2024. It is intended to honour her legacy and share her teachings. For more information, please see the attached poster and promotional images. You can register here: https://indigenous.iconproject.org/
