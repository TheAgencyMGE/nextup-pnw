Employer-led Workshop: RJC Engineers

UBC Career Centre

Join us for an interactive introduction to RJC, where you’ll get a quick overview of who we are, what we do, and the role structural engineering plays in shaping the built environment. Following the introduction, participants will take part in a hands-on team challenge inspired by a real-world engineering project. Working from a drawing of a building, teams will collaborate to develop their own structural framing solutions and share their ideas with the group. We’ll then walk through RJC’s actual approach, discussing the considerations, challenges, and decision-making process behind the design.
