Career Exploration: Intentional Exploration

UBC Okanagan Events

The role you think you might want: what do you actually know about the daily work, and where did that come from? This hour is about exploring options on purpose and testing them against real information and real people instead of guessing from job titles. In this hour we will cover: Where to find evidence about roles: labour market data, postings read as data, and people doing the work. How to run an informational conversation, including five questions worth asking in a twenty-minute call. Small experiments that test a path while you’re still a student: micro-internships, committees, volunteer work, side projects. A short exploration plan with real dates on it. Open to master’s and doctoral
