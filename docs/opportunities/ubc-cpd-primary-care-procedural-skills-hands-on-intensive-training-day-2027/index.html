UBC CPD: Primary Care Procedural Skills: Hands-On Intensive Training Day

Continuing Professional Development (UBC CPD)

Developed by the Nurse Practitioner CPD program, this intensive one-day training is designed to strengthen the procedural knowledge, confidence and clinical competence of primary care providers through a combination of focused didactic teaching and hands-on practice. Participants will learn evidence-informed approaches to patient assessment, procedural decision-making, technical skills and follow-up care for commonly encountered outpatient conditions.
