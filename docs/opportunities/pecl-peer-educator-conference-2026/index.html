PECL - Peer Educator Conference

Oregon State University Events

The conference is "For OSU Peer Educators, By OSU Peer Educators," the student leadership team wants to highlight peer educators from several roles and organizations. We will have student panels, discussions, and workshops with other OSU peer educators. The conference is about connecting with and learning from one another, both in terms of learning about campus resources and in terms of gaining professionally relevant knowledge from one another. At the end of the conference we have a panel conversation and celebration.
