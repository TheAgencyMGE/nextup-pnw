Pack your Confidence: An Interactive Career Workshop for Study Abroad Returnees

University of Oregon Events

Whether you studied abroad this past summer or a few years ago, your experience is worth talking about. Global Education Oregon (GEO) and the UO Career Center are partnering to help you reflect on your time abroad and learn how to market it with confidence. This interactive workshop offers resume support, help building your interview answers and elevator pitch, and research-backed guidance from career professionals to set you up for success after graduation. Open to all study abroad returnees, including GEO Center, faculty-led, internship, exchange, and third-party program participants. Your time abroad already set you apart. Now let's make it count.
