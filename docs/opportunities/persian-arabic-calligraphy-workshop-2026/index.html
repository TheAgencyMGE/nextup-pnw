Persian–Arabic Calligraphy Workshop

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Please join us for a hands-on Persian–Arabic Calligraphy Workshop on Friday, October 23, from 2:30–4:30 pm in Denny Hall 303. Featuring artists Elinor Holland and Reza Abbasi, the event will include live demonstrations and a chance to try calligraphy. Please help spread the word by sharing it with your students and colleagues. We hope to see you there!
