Communicating Your Research

Boise State University Events

Effectively sharing your research is just as important as conducting it! Join us for this interactive workshop designed to help students develop strong communication skills to present their research to various audiences. In this workshop, you’ll learn how to: Simplify complex ideas without losing their impact. Tailor your message to different audiences. Use engaging techniques to share your research effectively. Whether you’re preparing for a conference, writing about your findings, or simply want to improve how you communicate your work, this session is for you. All students are welcome come and enhance your research communication skills! This workshop is open only to Boise State students,
