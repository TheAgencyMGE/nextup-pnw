Notes on the Cold & Polar Futures: A Community Conversation (Session 1)

Oregon State University Events

This fall, students in an Oregon State University Marine Studies Honors College seminar led by Professor Anna Guasco, spent the term closely engaging with Notes on the Cold: Images of the Arctic and Antarctic . Join them as they share their research and creative responses to the exhibition through two special interactive events this fall: Session 1 on October 29, and Session 2 on November 19. Polar Futures: A Community Conversation offers a fresh way to experience the exhibition through hands-on activities and a creative guided exploration that considers what life in the polar regions can teach us about connection, community, and our shared future.
