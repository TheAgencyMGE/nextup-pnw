CCDPM Legacy Lecture: Inclusion, Exclusion and Porn: Centring (inter)subjective experiences in health-science scholarship

UBC Okanagan Events

The Centre for Chronic Disease Prevention and Management (CCDPM) is pleased to announce our next Legacy Lecture Series presentation at UBC Okanagan. Dr. Justin Haegele presents Inclusion, Exclusion, and Porn: Centring (inter)subjective experiences in health-science scholarship. Dr. Haegele will focus on inclusion and exclusion as (inter)subjective experiences, and the implications these conceptualizations have for health research. He will explore concepts related to epistemic injustice and inclusion porn, and discuss challenges in the field in which the concept of inclusion is used, sometimes without thinking about the needs, wants, and desires of people with disabilities. Bio Justin A. Haeg
