FCCS Visiting Artist Series

UBC Okanagan Events

The Visual Arts program brings in a diverse variety of visiting artists for campus talks and public gallery presentations that include visual artists, filmmakers, sculptors, painters, playwrights, essayists, and interactive and digital media artists. The 2026/27 Visiting Artist Series will be held in-person on Mondays at 3 pm in the University Theatre (ADM 026) as part of the CCS 250 Creative and Critical Art Theory class. All artist talks are free and open to all.
