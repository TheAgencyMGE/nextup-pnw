Networking & Small Talk: Building Connections in Canada

UBC Okanagan Events

Not sure how to start a conversation at a networking event? Looking to connect with others and grow your professional network? This interactive session will give you practical tips for networking and navigating small talk in the Canadian context, so you can feel more comfortable starting conversations, making connections and building relationships. You’ll learn strategies you can use right away to meet new people, grow your network and make the most of professional and social networking opportunities. Take the first step toward building meaningful connections in your new community! Register here This session will be facilitated by Kathy Crocker from the Connector Program.
