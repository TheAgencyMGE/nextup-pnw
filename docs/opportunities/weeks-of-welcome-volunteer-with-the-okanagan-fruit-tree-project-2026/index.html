Weeks of Welcome: Volunteer with the Okanagan Fruit Tree Project

UBC Okanagan Events

Join the Community Service Learning team and explore a bit more of Kelowna while contributing to a local non-profit! We are partnering with the Okanagan Fruit Tree Project for a morning of volunteering at their farm on Saturday, September 26 at 9 am. Learn more about how they support local food security and sustainability, and meet new people, all while giving back. Volunteers can take home some fresh food as a thank you for helping out. Open to: Domestic and international students of all disciplines. No previous experience or training required! Time commitment: ~3.5 hours on Saturday, September 26 from 9 am to 12:30 pm (including travel time). What to expect: You will volunteer with a group
