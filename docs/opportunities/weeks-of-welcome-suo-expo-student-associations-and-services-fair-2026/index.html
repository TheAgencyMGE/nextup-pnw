Weeks of Welcome: SUO Expo | Student Associations and Services Fair

UBC Okanagan Events

EXPO is back for 2026! Get ready to discover everything campus life has to offer. Meet SUO clubs and course unions, connect with fellow students, and find new ways to get involved in the UBC Okanagan community. Explore booths from sponsors, resource centres, and university partners, and learn about the services, events, and opportunities available to you throughout the year. UBCO students can also enjoy a free barbecue. Don’t forget your student card, as the team may request to see it for food service. Register your club for EXPO Hosted by the SUO.
