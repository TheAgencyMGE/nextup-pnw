Course-Based Programs Application Week at UBC Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies

Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies

UBC’s Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies (G+PS) is inviting prospective students to explore the university’s course-based and professional graduate programs during Course-Based Programs Application Week , running online from October 19 to 23, 2026. With more than 90 course-based programs spanning UBC’s faculties — from health and business to engineering, science, education, and the arts — the week gives prospective students the chance to meet program staff, ask questions in real time, and find the program that fits where they want to go next.
