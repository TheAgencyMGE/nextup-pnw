Civic Health Initiative Student Programming Convening

UW Tacoma Events

We are excited to invite you to the Civic Health Initiative Student Programming Convening , bringing together faculty, staff, and student leaders from across the University of Washington’s three campuses who are engaged in civic health and civic engagement. This is our fourth convening: We held convenings on the UW Seattle campus in January 2025, September 2025, and March 2026. This is the first convening on the Tacoma campus, and we are excited to introduce many to Tacoma's incredible campus community.
