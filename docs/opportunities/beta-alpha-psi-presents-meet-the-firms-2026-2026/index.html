Beta Alpha Psi Presents: Meet The Firms 2026

Seattle University Events

BAP Meet the Firms is an annual career and networking event hosted by the Seattle University Delta Eta Chapter of Beta Alpha Psi (BAP). Designed for accounting, finance, and information systems students, the event provides an opportunity to connect directly with recruiters from public accounting firms, corporations, and other industry organizations. Meet professionals, build your network, learn about career opportunities, and explore internships and full-time positions. Students of all class years and experience levels are encouraged to attend.
