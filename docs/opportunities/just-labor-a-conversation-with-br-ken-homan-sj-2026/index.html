Just Labor: A Conversation with Br. Ken Homan, SJ

"Is It Just Labor? A Catholic Conversation" Join Jesuit Brother and St. Louis University Assistant Professor of History Ken Homan, SJ, for a wide ranging-conversation about the history of Jesuits in the labor movement, the current state of Catholic labor activism, and the future of labor in the age of AI. All are welcome to join this important and interactive conversation about how Catholic social teaching can influence how we look at labor, and learn about how Jesuits have been involved in labor movements in the United State and around the world. Free and open to the public. Light refreshments will be served! RSVPs are appreciated but not required. Thursday, October 22, 2026 4pm - 5:15pm Ca
