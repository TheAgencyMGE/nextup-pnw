UBC CPD: Women’s Health Update

Continuing Professional Development (UBC CPD)

The Victoria Society of Obstetrics and Gynecology is pleased to present the annual Women’s Health Update . This hybrid conference brings together health care providers from Vancouver Island, the Lower Mainland, and beyond for a full day of evidence-based education, practical clinical updates, and professional networking. Featuring expert speakers and a diverse program covering a broad range of women’s health topics, the conference is designed to support health care providers in delivering high-quality, up-to-date care for patients across British Columbia and Canada. Location: Victoria Conference Centre, 720 Douglas St, Victoria, BC
