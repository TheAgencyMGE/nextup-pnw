Virtual Application Workshop

Oregon State University Events

Applying to college can feel overwhelming, but we're here to help. Join Oregon State University Ecampus enrollment specialists for a live virtual application workshop designed for prospective undergraduate students interested in earning their degree online. We'll walk through the application process step by step, highlight common questions and requirements, and provide guidance to help you submit a strong application. Participants will also have the opportunity to ask questions and receive real-time support from our enrollment services team.
