Discover Undergraduate Research: A Virtual Event for Ecampus Students

Oregon State University Events

Are you an Ecampus student curious about undergraduate research but unsure where to start? Join us for an informative and engaging virtual event! Whether you're exploring your interests, looking to build your resume, or preparing for graduate school, undergraduate research can open doors to exciting opportunities. In this session, you'll: Learn about the URSA Engage program. Learn what undergraduate research is and why it matters. Discover how to find research opportunities — no matter where you're located. No prior research experience is required; just bring your curiosity and questions! Registration required: Sign up now!
