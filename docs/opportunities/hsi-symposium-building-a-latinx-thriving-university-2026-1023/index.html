HSI Symposium: Building a Latinx-Thriving University

University of Oregon Events

Join the University of Oregon for the HSI Symposium: Building a Latinx-Thriving University . This two-day gathering brings campus and community together to explore how UO can strengthen support, deepen belonging, and expand opportunities for current and prospective Latiné students. As UO explores its pathway toward becoming a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) , the symposium will build on the scholarship, student leadership, cultural initiatives, and community partnerships already shaping the university.
