HMSC Research Seminar: Beyond Detection: Whale Behavior, Biologging, and Ocean Intelligence at Leviathan Systems

Oregon State University Events

Speaker: Nathan Krajewski, founder and CEO of Leviathan Systems Topic: A whale appearing at the surface is one moment in a much larger story. What is that animal doing beneath the water? What is happening in the ocean around it? And how can we connect the observations needed to begin answering those questions? At Leviathan Systems, these questions are driving the development of an approach to ocean observation that brings cameras, animal-borne sensors, underwater sound, environmental data, and artificial intelligence together.
