Masterclass with Boise State Alumnus - Ian Kelly, tenor

Boise State University Events

Boise State alumnus Ian Kelly returns to campus to lead a vocal masterclass with Boise State students. A member of the United States Army Chorus, the U.S. Army’s premier vocal ensemble, Ian Kelly serves as assistant enlisted conductor and tenor soloist, performing in support of the President of the United States and other senior government and military leaders in Washington, D.C. Join us as Ian works with selected Boise State voice students, offering guidance and insights drawn from his professional performing career.
