Dissertation Defence: Biosynthesis of Spirooxindole Alkaloids

UBC Okanagan Events

Minh Tuan Anh Nguyen, supervised by Dr. Thuy Dang, will defend their dissertation titled “Biosynthesis of Spirooxindole Alkaloids” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy in Chemistry. An abstract for Minh Tuan Anh Nguyen’s dissertation is included below. Examinations are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person exams.
