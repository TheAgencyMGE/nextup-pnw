Knowledge Synthesis Workshop Series Part 2: Translating Database Searches and Finding Grey Literature

UBC Library Research Commons

This workshop will cover key steps in creating a comprehensive search when conducting a knowledge synthesis or systematic review study. Topics to be covered in this session: Translate a search strategy in multiple databases How to plan and execute your grey literature search How to document your search There will be time to ask questions throughout the workshop. Please bring any questions you have! This workshop will be recorded. Zoom link will be sent to registrants two days before the event and again one hour before the event.
