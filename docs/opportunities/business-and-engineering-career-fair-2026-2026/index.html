Business and Engineering Career Fair 2026

Seattle University Events

The Annual Business and Engineering Career Fair hosts around 40 employers who want to engage with Seattle University students about job and internship opportunities focused around business and engineering. Students interested in attending should look at Handshake to register. Interested Employers should contact Joana Borges at jborges@seattleu.edu. About the Albers Career Center The Albers Career Center creates opportunities to connect students and the business community. We provide job search preparation assistance, information on job and internship opportunities, employer resources, and professional development programs.
