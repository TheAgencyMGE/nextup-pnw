Ten for Teaching: Providing timely learning-focused feedback to students (ASYNCHRONOUS)

Boise State University Events

Learning-focused feedback is feedback that’s given in support of student learning and focuses on specific, actionable guidance that students can use to move closer to the learning outcomes. In this workshop, we’ll explore not only why this is helpful to students but also how to do it in our own courses. One strategy is to use rubrics which communicate the standards to students and also help us provide feedback about where they need to focus future efforts. We will explore a variety of rubrics and analyze how they can help us communicate this learning-focused feedback. Finally, you will be able to start building your own rubrics.
