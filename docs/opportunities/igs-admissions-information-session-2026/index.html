IGS Admissions Information Session

UBC Okanagan Events

Interested in pursuing graduate studies through UBC Okanagan’s Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies (IGS) program ? Join us for an online IGS Admissions Information Session to learn more about the admissions requirements and application process. Led by Dr. Christine Schreyer , the session will provide prospective applicants with an overview of how to apply to IGS, what to consider when preparing an application, and the steps involved in the admissions process. Following the presentation, participants will have an opportunity to ask questions and hear from a panel of UBC Okanagan representatives with expertise in the IGS program and graduate admissions.
