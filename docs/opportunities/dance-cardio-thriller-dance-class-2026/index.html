Dance Cardio Thriller: Dance Class

UW Bothell Events

Join ARC Fitness for A Dance Cardio class and learn the famous Thriller Dance choreography. Dance Cardio is a type of exercise that combines high-energy dance movements with music. It is a fun and effective cardiovascular workout that helps build endurance and stamina. Our Group fitness classes are open to all abilities, skill levels, and genders. Group Fitness Instructors provide modifications for all levels and all abilities to ensure that everyone can take a step toward meeting their fitness goals. Our ARC certified Group Fitness Instructors are top-quality, providing a workout experience that is sure to captivate you! Register online 14 days in advance through myarc.uwb.edu or in-person
