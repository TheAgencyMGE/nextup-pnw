Exploring Different Writing Tools: Introduction to LaTeX

UBC Okanagan Events

If you’re a graduate student or postdoc who spends significant time writing or editing text, then it is highly likely you’ve encountered and struggled with the limitations of traditional word processors like Microsoft Word or Google Docs. This workshop will focus on introducing LaTeX, a professional typesetting system that allows you to focus on content while automatically handling your formatting requirements. We’ll cover what LaTeX is and how it differs from more common writing tools by exploring its advantages for various writing tasks and breaking down the basic components of a LaTeX document.
