Study Abroad Fair

University of Oregon Events

Are you interested in studying abroad? Stop by GEO 's study abroad fair to find a program for you and be entered to win a $500 GEO program scholarship! Speak to a program advisor, hear about the student experience from a program alum, learn more about study abroad financial aid and scholarships, and pick up some snacks and GEO swag. This event is part of International Education Month (IEM) programming. View the full calendar of IEM events here: https://international.uoregon.edu/IEM .
