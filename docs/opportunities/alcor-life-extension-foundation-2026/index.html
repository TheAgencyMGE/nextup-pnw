Alcor Life Extension Foundation

UW Bothell Events

Alcor Life Extension Foundation, is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Scottsdale, Arizona. Founded in 1972, Alcor has spent over five decades pioneering research in organ, tissue, and brain preservation technologies. Today, much of our focus is on addressing the national organ transplant crisis by exploring ways to extend organ viability and ultimately help save more lives! We are hosting an industry information session for students interested in molecular biology, physiology, nursing, engineering, neuroscience, and related fields.
