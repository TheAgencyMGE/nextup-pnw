Thesis Defence: ‘Desecting’ in a Post-Assad Syria

UBC Okanagan Events

Mariam Al Barazi, supervised by Dr. Adam Jones, will defend their thesis titled “’Desecting’ in a Post-Assad Syria” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Arts in Interdisciplinary Graduate Studies – Global Studies Theme. An abstract for Mariam Al Barazi’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person defences.
