Feast of Creation in Christ - Ecumenical Worship Gathering

Join with Ecumenical leaders in celebrating the new Feast of Creation in Christ in an ecumenical worship service. While the Feast of Creation has been an established Christian festival day for many years, this year marks the first inclusion of this Festival day in the Revised Common Lectionary, the 3-year cycle of readings used by most Christian communities. Sponsored by the CEIE Faith & Ecology Roundtable in partnership with local Christian communions, we celebrate this occasion with an ecumenical worship gathering.
