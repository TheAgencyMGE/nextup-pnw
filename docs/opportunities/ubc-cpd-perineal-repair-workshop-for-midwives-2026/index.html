UBC CPD: Perineal Repair Workshop for Midwives

Continuing Professional Development (UBC CPD)

Perineal trauma is a common obstetrical event. However, midwives make every effort to avoid it and many report very rates. Many midwives also identify a need for more clinical experience and review of perineal repair techniques. The Perineal Repair Workshop, held at the University of Victoria, provides hands-on practice in these key approaches.
