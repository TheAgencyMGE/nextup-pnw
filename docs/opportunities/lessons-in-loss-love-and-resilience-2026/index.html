Lessons in Loss, Love, and Resilience

Oregon State University Events

Join us for a lecture on grief and resilience with Arik Housley. When a community loses people it loves, the weight doesn’t stay in one place. It shows up in classrooms, friendships and quiet moments. Oregon State University – Cascades invites students, faculty, staff and neighbors to come together for an honest conversation about grief with author and speaker Arik Housley. Housley lost his daughter, Alaina, in a 2018 shooting in Southern California. He turned that loss into Always November: Lessons in Loss, Love, and Resilience and a message that talks about grief without feeling heavy-handed or clinical.
