Responding to Disclosures of Sexualized Violence

UBC Okanagan Events

Presented by SVPRO & the GSA Join us for an interactive workshop facilitated by Education Specialists from the Sexual Violence Prevention & Response Office (SVPRO), open to all graduate students. This workshop will foster the skills necessary to support survivors of sexualized and gender-based violence. Survivors often disclose to someone they already trust, such as a friend, mentor or even a faculty member. It is important to develop the skills to respond in a supportive manner without causing further harm. Participants will also learn about various resources for support on campus and relevant policies.
