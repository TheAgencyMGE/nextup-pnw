Thesis Defence: Interference Patterns

UBC Okanagan Events

Christine McPhee, supervised by Dr. Matt Rader, will defend their thesis titled “Interference Patterns: a Messy Manuscript, Failing to Assemble into Sense” in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Fine Arts in Creative Writing. An abstract for Christine McPhee’s thesis is included below. Defences are open to all members of the campus community as well as the general public. Registration is not required for in-person defences.
