CHSPR/CAE Seminar | Role Commitment and the Institutional Standing to Direct

W. Maurice Young Centre for Applied Ethics

Speaker: Daniel Telech, University of the Fraser Valley People who occupy institutional roles — clinical directors, hospital administrators, public-health officials — routinely direct others to comply with policies and codes of conduct that they may not personally live up to. This talk asks when such directives retain their authority and when a leader’s own conduct undermines it, arguing that what matters is whether the norm the leader has violated is constitutive of the role’s defining commitment (a fiduciary or integrity duty, say) or merely incidental to it (the leader’s own health habits, say). The account offers a principled way to understand when “for cause” accountability is warranted
