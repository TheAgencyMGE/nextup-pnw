Make Your Major Work For You: MS in Management

University of Oregon Events

Graduating in the next two years and wondering what comes next? Learn how an MS in Management (MSM) can turn your degree into a career. This one-year graduate program from the Lundquist College of Business builds the business, leadership, and networking skills that open doors to your dream job. Stop by Chapman Library at the Clark Honors College to chat with: Emily Moore , program director, MS in Management Jessica Best , director of career strategy, Mohr Career Services Andrew Fudge , assistant director of graduate admissions, Lundquist College of Business CHC students will also hear about their guaranteed admissions pathway to the MSM.
