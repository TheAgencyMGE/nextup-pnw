Cultivating Interview Skills for CAS Students

Oregon State University Events

First impressions matter, but interviews can be intimidating, especially when you don't know what to expect. If you're wondering how to make the best first impression at your next agricultural interview, this workshop is a great place to start. Join us to learn the basics of employer expectations around appearance, etiquette, and communication throughout the interview process. We'll also talk about how to answer interview questions using the STAR method, and practice answering questions in a supportive environment.
