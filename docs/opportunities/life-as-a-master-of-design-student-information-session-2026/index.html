Life as a Master of Design Student Information Session

UBC Okanagan Events

This session is designed to give prospective students a closer, more candid look at the Master of Design (MDes) program experience through the voices of those currently enrolled in the program. Panellists will include students who joined directly from undergraduate programs and those who came with prior industry experience, providing a well-rounded perspective on academic life, personal growth, and the transition into the program. Topics will range from day-to-day student life and strategies for success, to community, campus culture, and what it means to be a graduate student in the Okanagan.
