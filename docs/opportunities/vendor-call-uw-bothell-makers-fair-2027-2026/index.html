Vendor Call - UW Bothell Makers Fair 2027

UW Bothell Events

APPLY TO VEND HERE!: https://tinyurl.com/uwbmake Calling all makers, artists, and creatives! Share your talents with the UW community and sell your crafts at our 4th UW Bothell Makers Fair on Wednesday, February 10th, 2027 from 12:00 pm - 4:00 pm located at the ARC Overlook and North Creek Events Center (NCEC) at the University of Washington Bothell Campus. Vendors will be provided with 6 ft. (full) or 3 ft. (shared) of table space, tablecloth, and 1-2 chairs. Access to power is limited and requests will be considered on a first-come, first-served basis. All vendors are responsible for their point-of-sale systems and the safety and security of their items.
