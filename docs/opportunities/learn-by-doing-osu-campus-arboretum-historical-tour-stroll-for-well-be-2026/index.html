Learn by Doing: OSU Campus Arboretum Historical Tour & Stroll for Well-Being

Oregon State University Events

Learn by Doing is your opportunity to get out into campus, nature or the community, get to know other new students, and learn while you’re doing it! No matter which Learn by Doing experience you choose, you'll make memories and build new connections to Beaver Nation. Learn by Doing experiences are open to new incoming first-year and transfer students. For fall 2026, experiences run between Sept. 18 and Nov. 4, and registration opens June 15. Join a guided walk through the OSU Campus Arboretum that blends campus history, plant diversity, and themes of balance, change, and belonging.
