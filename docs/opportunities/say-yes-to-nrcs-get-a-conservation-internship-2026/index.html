Say Yes to NRCS: Get A Conservation Internship

Oregon State University Events

Are you interested in conservation work through the federal government? Are you interested in sustainable agriculture and creative solutions to mitigate climate change while caring for our public lands? If so, working with the Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) through the US Department of Agriculture might be a great opportunity for you! Join a USDA-NRCS employee/OSU alum to learn about open internships with the NRCS for 2027. Learn about the internship program, how to apply, and important details to help you get hired with the NRCS. Join our virtual session at: https://oregonstate.zoom.us/meeting/register/Pt7YKFkwRaewOM9ZYmnr2w
