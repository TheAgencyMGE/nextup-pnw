Proposing a 100-Level UF Course Info Sessions

Boise State University Events

As previously announced, University Foundations will be accepting proposals for new and renewing 100-level UF courses this fall. Approved courses will be offered at least annually during the upcoming three-year cycle: Fall 2027 through Summer 2030. Boise State’s 100-level University Foundations courses (UF 101, UF 102, etc.) are meant to introduce entering students to college and college-level inquiry, helping them become more active and expert learners as they investigate and communicate about important ideas that affect everyone. Want to Learn more? Join us at an upcoming information session: September 14, 10:00 am - 11:00 am, Zoom only September 16, 3:00 pm - 4:00 pm, Hybrid in ILC 315 Se
