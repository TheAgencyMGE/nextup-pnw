Career Insights from Lundquist College Alumni

University of Oregon Events

Wondering about life after college? Join Mohr Career Services for a casual career chat with Lundquist College alumni who've been exactly where you are. These alumni have experience as entrepreneurs, analysts, managers, community bankers, consultants, and more, and they're ready to share what actually happened to them after graduation. Ask about their career paths, early‑career decisions, industry life, or any other questions you have. Alumni will host open‑conversation prompts such as: What I wish I knew at 19 How I figured out what I wanted to do And more. Come for the stories and stay for some clarity!
