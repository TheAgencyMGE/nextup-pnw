Thesis Writing: Dissecting the Discussion

UBC Okanagan Events

Have you ever found it challenging to answer the critical “so what” question in your research? Join our online workshop, focusing on the discussion section of your thesis or article. We’ll dissect the essential components and elevate your writing style to effectively convey the significance and impact of your work. Bring your questions and writings for insightful feedback and discussion. This session is a must-attend for graduate students across all fields seeking to elevate their research narrative! This workshop qualifies for the Scholarly Research, Writing, and Publishing Credential offered through the College of Graduate Studies. REGISTER NOW For a complete list of upcoming CSC workshops
