Mathematics Invitational

Boise State University Events

This fun annual event is a celebration of creativity, problem-solving, and the joy of doing math together. Join us as a volunteer or share this event with local high schoolers that might be interested! Teams of high school students are invited to Boise State to compete in team and individual mathematics competitions with the opportunity to earn awards and prizes. In the afternoon, Dr. Po-Shen Loh ( poshenloh.com ) of Carnegie Mellon University will speak about problem solving and the power of human ingenuity in the age of AI.
