Growing your Career in Plant & Soil Sciences

Oregon State University Events

Calling all new and returning Ecampus Crop & Soil Science, Botany, and Horticulture students! Curious about where your plant or soil sciences degree will take you? Join us to learn from employers and alumni about their experiences, insights, and advice for growing your career in plant sciences. During the panel discussion, you’ll hear about our employers and alumni’s pathways into their current roles and gain advice on how to network and find work within the industry. Following the panel, you’ll be invited to optional virtual breakout rooms where you can ask further questions of our panelists and engage in informal networking.
