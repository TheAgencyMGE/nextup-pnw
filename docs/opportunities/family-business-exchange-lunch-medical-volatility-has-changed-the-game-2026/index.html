Family Business Exchange | Lunch: Medical Volatility Has Changed the Game

Seattle University Events

The Family Business Exchange is joined by Katrina Johnson from Gallagher (formerly Assured Partners) to discuss Medical Volatility - Why Employer Cost Strategy Must Evolve. In this lunchtime workshop, Katrina will touch on what is driving medical trends and how employers should think differently about their benefits looking forward to 2027. About the Family Business Exchange Seattle University’s Family Business Exchange (FBX) is a community of Pacific Northwest family business owners who gather to engage in education, networking, and professional development tailored specifically to their needs.
