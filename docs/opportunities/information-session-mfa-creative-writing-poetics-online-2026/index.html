Information Session: MFA Creative Writing & Poetics (online)

UW Bothell Events

Join us for an information session to learn more about the Master of Fine Arts in Creative Writing & Poetics at the University of Washington Bothell. Our innovative program encourages writers to experiment across genres, forms and media During the session, you’ll learn about the MFA curriculum, faculty and creative community, program highlights, admissions process and more. You’ll also have an opportunity to ask questions to help you determine whether the program is the right fit for your creative goals. Whether you’re ready to apply or just beginning to explore graduate study in creative writing, we invite you to join us and learn more.
