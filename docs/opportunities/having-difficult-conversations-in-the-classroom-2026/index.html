Having Difficult Conversations in the Classroom

UBC Okanagan Events

Presented by SVPRO & the GSA Join us for an interactive workshop facilitated by Education Specialists from the Sexual Violence Prevention & Response Office (SVPRO), open to all graduate students. This workshop will offer pedagogical frameworks for engaging in a wide range of topics in the classroom, from controversial issues to trauma-related content. We will cover practical tools for fostering safety and inclusion in the classroom in order to promote participatory learning and critical reflection. This workshop is offered as part of the UBCO Graduate Student Association’s Graduate Student Advocacy Workshops. These workshops offer students the opportunity to participate in monthly, free work
