Data Bites – Introduction to Data Management Plans (DMPs)

UBC Library Research Commons

Learn how to create effective and funder-compliant Data Management Plans (DMPs) in this hands-on workshop led by UBC’s Research Data Management team , designed for graduate students and researchers across disciplines. Participants will explore best practices in research data planning and gain experience using the DMP Assistant tool , a free, bilingual platform maintained by the Digital Research Alliance of Canada. This session uses UBC’s Simplified DMP Template to make data management planning accessible and practical.
