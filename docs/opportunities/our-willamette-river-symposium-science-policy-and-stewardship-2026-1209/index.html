Our Willamette River Symposium: Science, Policy, and Stewardship

Oregon State University Events

Symposium Website: https://ourwillamette.oregonstate.edu/ Now in its third year, this symposium brings together an exceptional mix of restoration practitioners, utilities, landowners, researchers, Tribal members, and public agencies to share insights, build partnerships, and apply knowledge to achieve meaningful on-the-ground outcomes for the Willamette River watershed. The program will feature plenary presentations, an evening poster session, and facilitated discussions. For more information, including how to help sponsor the gathering, contact us at ourwillamette@gmail.com
