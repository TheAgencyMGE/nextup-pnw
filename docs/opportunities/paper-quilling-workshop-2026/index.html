Paper Quilling Workshop

King County Library System

Transform paper strips into elegant art! Explore the delicate and mesmerizing world of paper quilling, where simple strips of paper are curled, shaped, and arranged to create stunning designs. In this hands-on workshop, you’ll learn fundamental quilling techniques—coils, scrolls, shapes, and patterns—and discover how these simple elements can be combined into intricate flowers, motifs, and decorative artwork. Paper quilling is relaxing, tactile, and wonderfully satisfying, making it perfect for adults who enjoy slow, detailed craftwork.
