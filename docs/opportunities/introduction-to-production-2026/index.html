Introduction to Production

UBC Studios

How do they get such great footage? Are you a UBC Instructor or Staff, and planning on filming your own video content? This in-person workshop will provide advice for DIY camera, lighting and audio. Media professionals will share tips, tricks, and best practices. Whether you’re filming outside, in an office, or in a professional studio, we can help you move from “good enough” to media that you’ll be proud of. Presented by UBC Studios in collaboration with the UBC Digital Media Community of Practice Led by Nick Miller, Chris Spencer and Michael Sider of UBC Studios
