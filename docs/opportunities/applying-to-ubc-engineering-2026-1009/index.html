Applying to UBC Engineering

UBC Okanagan Events

Future students are invited to learn more about applying to UBC Engineering at the Okanagan and Vancouver campuses. See what your future as a UBC Engineering student might look like and learn about course planning, admission requirements and the application process! Information covered will be applicable to both domestic and international students. Parents, guardians, teachers and counsellors are also welcome to attend. Please note: The information covered in this session will be the same content covered in the October 8 evening session. Join us on Zoom on October 9 at 8:30 am Pacific Time. Register Here
