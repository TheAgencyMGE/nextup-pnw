River School | Nepal, Spring 2027 Info Session

University of Washington Campus Calendar

River School | Nepal is an interdisciplinary study abroad program based in Kathmandu and the West Seti Karnali River Basin. The program explores the impacts of climate change and contemporary development on river ecologies and communities. It fosters aquatic biodiversity and human well-being at a local scale through participatory engagement. UW students will collaborate with Nepali students and local stakeholders to explore opportunities for regenerative watershed planning and co-design a project with tangible impacts on ecological resilience and community health.
