Community Cooking Classes: Master Chef Cooking Competition

UBC Okanagan Events

Join us for a special edition of the Picnic Cooking Classes – Master Chef Cooking Competition! How it works: Each participant will have their own cooking station with a secret mystery ingredient they must use to create a unique dish. Participants have the opportunity to grab pantry staples and produce and use their creativity to put together their dish. Participants have one hour to create their dish. Workshops feature mostly vegetarian meals, and we do our best to accommodate dietary preferences and restrictions.* All skill levels are welcome — whether you’re a complete beginner or just looking to sharpen your skills.
