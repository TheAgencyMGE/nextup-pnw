Introduction to Open Educational Resources (OER) (ZOOM)

Boise State University Events

Unlock the power of Open Educational Resources (OER) in this introductory workshop. Together we will gain a comprehensive understanding of OER, from its definition to its benefits and challenges. You will also learn how to navigate copyright, fair use, and Creative Commons licensing and explore strategies for creating high-quality and engaging OER aligned with your teaching goals. After participating in this workshop, you will be able to: Define OER Describe the challenges and benefits of OER creation and use Interpret and apply copyright, fair use, and creative commons licensing --- Event Details: https://boisestate.campusgroups.com/rsvp?id=397151
