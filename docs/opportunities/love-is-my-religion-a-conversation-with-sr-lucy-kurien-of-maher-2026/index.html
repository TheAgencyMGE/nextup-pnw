Love is My Religion: A Conversation with Sr. Lucy Kurien of Maher

The CEIE Interreligious Women Leader's Roundtable host this presentation and conversation with Sr. Lucy Kurien, founder of Maher, a faith-based NGO in India. Sr. Lucy proclaims that "love is my religion", the guiding principle of Maher's work: to provide shelter, relief, education, outreach and education among India's most vulnerable populations. Join us for an afternoon tea and conversation, as Sr. Lucy shares her insights about religion and her call to help the poorest of the poor and particularly, abused and traumatized women.
