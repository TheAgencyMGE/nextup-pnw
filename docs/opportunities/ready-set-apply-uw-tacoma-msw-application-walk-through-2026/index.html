Ready, Set, Apply: UW Tacoma MSW Application Walk-Through

UW Tacoma Events

Applying to graduate school can feel overwhelming—but you do not have to figure out the application alone. Join the UW Tacoma School of Social Work & Criminal Justice for a live, step-by-step walk-through of the 3-Year MSW application for autumn 2027. We will show you how to locate the correct program, navigate the online application, prepare your required materials, designate recommenders, upload transcripts, and avoid common mistakes. This session is designed for prospective students who are preparing to apply or have already started an application. There will also be time for questions.
