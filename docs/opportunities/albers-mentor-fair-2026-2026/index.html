Albers Mentor Fair 2026

Seattle University Events

The Mentor Fair is a key event in the Albers Mentor Program which provides graduate level and senior undergraduate business students with the unique opportunity to interact with upper-level executives from leading Puget Sound companies and organizations. Small groups of one to three students meet monthly with a mentor throughout the academic year. Students can develop business contacts, access industry information, and gain valuable insights from experienced and successful professionals. Mentors have the opportunity to give back and support students in their career goals and professional development.
