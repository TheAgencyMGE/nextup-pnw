Engagement@OSU Webinar ~ Learning to follow: Collaborating with Tribes on dam removal science in the Klamath Basin

Oregon State University Events

Event Announcement Summary Join OSU Extension and Engagement as we welcome Desiree Tullos for a presentation on the historic Klamath River dam removals, the world's largest river restoration project, completed in 2024 through the leadership of basin Tribes. She will provide an overview of the project and share lessons learned from OSU's collaboration with Tribal partners, highlighting the importance of reciprocity, trust-building, intellectual property, and adaptive approaches in conducting science that supports meaningful restoration outcomes.
