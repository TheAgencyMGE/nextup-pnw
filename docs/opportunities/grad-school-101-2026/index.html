Grad School 101

Boise State University Events

Are you wrapping up your time at Boise State and potentially considering attending graduate school? Feeling overwhelmed by the process? COBE Career Services and the Graduate College are here to help! Join us as we discuss the graduate school application process and tips and tricks for applicants. Bring your questions, and we'll bring the snacks! All majors/disciplines are welcome. You will walk away with... A timeline for when to apply to graduate school An overview of required application materials and how to tackle them Tips and tricks for organizing your apps Support resources for next steps Free snacks😉 Grad school can be overwhelming, but we're here to help!
