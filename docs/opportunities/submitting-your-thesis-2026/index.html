Working on your graduate thesis? Registration Information Registration is open to current UBC graduate students. After registering, you will receive a confirmation email at the e-mail associated with your community.grad.ubc.ca account. If you experience any difficulty using the online registration tool, please e-mail us at graduate.pathways@ubc.ca . Please email us if you are registered and are no longer able to attend this event.
