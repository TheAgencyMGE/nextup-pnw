Weeks of Welcome: Late Arrival International Student Orientation

UBC Okanagan Events

Missed Jump Start or arrived at UBCO after the main orientation? This session is designed to help you get settled and feel more confident as you begin your journey at UBCO and in Canada. The session covers many of the same key topics introduced during Jump Start orientation, including immigration requirements and maintaining your study permit status, working in Canada, health insurance, Canadian classroom culture and academic expectations and language development supports. You’ll also learn about housing, banking, transportation and everyday life in Kelowna, as well as culturally specific adjustment strategies, community-building opportunities, and international student advising and wellness
