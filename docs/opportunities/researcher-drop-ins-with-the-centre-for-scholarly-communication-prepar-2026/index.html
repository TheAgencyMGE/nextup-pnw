Researcher Drop-Ins with the Centre for Scholarly Communication: Preparing Graduate School Applications

UBC Okanagan Events

Researchers are invited to meet your Okanagan research support team! The Centre for Scholarly Communication (CSC) will be hosting weekly drop-ins to help answer your questions and guide your research. Are you preparing a graduate school application? Not sure where to start? Looking to receive feedback? Join us this week to ask questions about writing a successful graduate school application . There will be no presentation, so please come prepared with questions. Come by to chat, learn more, or ask questions! No registration is needed. Stop by for a beverage or snack and to receive support with your grad school application! Register here Questions? Contact the CSC at csc.ok@ubc.ca .
