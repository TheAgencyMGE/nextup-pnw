Geosciences Seminar: Jodi Brandt

Boise State University Events

Please join us Monday, August 31th at 3PM for Jodi Brandt's seminar. The seminar topic is " Beaver rewilding and beyond: Decision-support for managing “rangewaters” in the western United States " The seminar will be held in BGYM 219 at 3PM . There will be coffee and cookies in the Lobby of ERB prior to the talk at 2:40 PM . We will be holding a Pizza Lunch at 12pm in ERB 1161! More on Jodi's seminar below: Jodi Brandt Title Beaver rewilding and beyond: Decision-support for managing “rangewaters” in the western United States Abstract Rangeland waters – hereafter rangewaters - are natural water sources in rangelands, including headwater streams, riparian zones, and wet meadows.
