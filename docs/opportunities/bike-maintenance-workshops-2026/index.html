Bike Maintenance Workshops

UBC Okanagan Events

Learn basic bike repairs from a bike mechanic at McGee Cycles, or ask a question, during this hands-on bike maintenance workshop and demonstration. Bring your own bike with you, and it may be used for a demo (plus get some quick fixes). Two sessions are available from 11 am to noon and noon to 1 pm, although drop-ins anytime are welcome. Please RSVP in advance to help us plan this event. Coffee and light snacks will be provided. RSVP here This session is one of several UBCO GoByBike Week events taking place from September 19 to 25.
