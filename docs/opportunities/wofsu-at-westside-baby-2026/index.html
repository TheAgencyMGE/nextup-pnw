WofSU at WestSide Baby

Seattle University Events

Join Women of SU at WestSide Baby. WestSide Baby is an organization dedicated to providing essential items to keep children safe, warm, and dry by collecting and distributing diapers, clothing, and equipment needed for children and families throughout Western King County. Volunteers will be asked to spend a couple hours going through and organizing donated goods so they can be redistributed to families in need. Spots are limited. Volunteers must be 18+ years of age. Questions? Contact Ellen Montanana at montanan14@gmail.com .
