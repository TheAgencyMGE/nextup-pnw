APRU: SCL Webinar Sustainable Cities and Landscapes in Real Estate

University of Oregon Events

The second Association of Pacific Rim Universities Sustainable Cities and Landscapes Program webinar in our series will explore the future of sustainable real estate and urban development titled, "Sustainable Cities and Landscapes in Real Estate." Hear from experts from Universiti Malaya, University of the West of England, and University of New South Wales, Sydney, as they discuss research and innovation in the built environment, the impact of AI-driven data centers on urban sustainability, and the risks and opportunities that carbon policy uncertainty and ESG trends present for sustainable property markets.
