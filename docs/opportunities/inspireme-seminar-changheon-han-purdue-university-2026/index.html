While offering unprecedented production opportunities in fields such as biotechnology, semiconductors, and optics, both need adaptability to new scenarios and accessibility for non-experts. In-space manufacturing further requires autonomy under resource constraints. This talk discusses a vision that connects manufacturing and space through intelligent manufacturing, extending beyond any particular machine, factory, environment, or resource base while keeping people at its center.
