Information session: Applying for Limited Submission Opportunities

University of Washington Campus Calendar

Applying for Limited Submission Opportunities Date: Oct. 7, 2026 Time: 12:30 – 1:30 p.m. Location: Online (Zoom link will be sent upon registration) Register today for this session. Many federal agencies and foundations offer grants, awards, and fellowships that limit the number of applications that can come from one institution. In order to increase the chances of the UW succeeding in our Limited Submission Opportunities (LSOs) applications , the Office of Research established a procedure to review pre-proposals in a timely fashion. In this information session, participants learn how LSOs are managed at UW and get tips for navigating the internal application process. Hosted by Cecilia Giach
